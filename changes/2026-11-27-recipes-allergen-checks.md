# Allergens from recipes: each dish's declaration checked against what it is made of

- Each supplier price on the Inventory page now carries its product's allergens: which of the 14 it contains and may contain, when they were put in and where from (the spec sheet and its date). Two suppliers' products can differ, so each price keeps its own. Until someone saves them they show as not known yet, never as free from allergens.
- Pepper can read a product's allergens off its spec sheet (a PDF or a photo): it shows what it read for each of the 14 with the words from the sheet, and nothing is saved until you check it and tap Save. In the chat, attach the sheet and ask Pepper to read the product's allergens: it shows a card and saves on Confirm.
- The Recipes page has an Allergens tab: every food dish on your menus with what it declares against what its recipes suggest (the allergens of the supplier products each venue orders, through its prep recipes and what its options add), dishes with open differences first. "The recipe has sesame; the dish does not say so", "The dish declares celery; nothing in the recipe has it", with the evidence (which ingredient, through which prep recipe or option, and which supplier's product) and what isn't known yet. Pick a venue to see what its own suppliers' products suggest.
- A dish where nothing differs but some ingredients' allergens aren't known yet shows Not all known, never Agrees: until the supplier products have their allergens, that is most dishes. Agrees means every ingredient is known and matches.
- Accept (the differences you tick) changes the dish's allergens in the menu's draft, exactly as the Allergen matrix saves them; guests see it once the menu's allergens are published. Dismiss keeps the declaration with a note saying why, and holds until the differences change. Nothing changes what a dish declares by itself.
- The recipe editor shows each recipe's suggested allergens at each venue.
- Pepper answers "does the pad thai's declaration match its recipe?" with the differences and the evidence. Allergy questions from guests are still answered from what the live menu declares.
- Reports: the starters Dishes with allergen differences and Items with unknown allergens, the subject Allergen checks, and each product's allergens on Supplier prices.
- The liability note is on the tab and every check: your business is responsible for what each dish declares, and the check is only as good as the supplier data under it.
