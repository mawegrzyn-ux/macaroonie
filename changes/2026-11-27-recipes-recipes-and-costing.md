# Recipes, and what each one costs at every venue

- A new Recipes page (Stock, after Purchasing): every dish and prep recipe (a sauce, a marinade, a batch) with what goes into it, the method and a photo. Prep recipes go inside other recipes, up to five deep; a recipe that would end up inside itself is refused and the page names the loop.
- What a recipe costs at each venue's own supplier prices, per portion for a dish and per litre, kilo or portion for prep: priced at today's prices, the last price paid or the average paid over 90 days, side by side. The cost follows the lines as you change them, before you save.
- How it is worked out, step by step: each line's quantity, the price it was costed at and where that price came from, the yield, the total and the division by portions.
- Fully, partly or not costed: anything that can't be costed (no supplier price, no size for a unit such as a slice or a tbsp) is listed with how to fix it, and the list can show just those recipes.
- Variations: Make a variation copies a recipe as a version of it (a takeaway version, one venue's own), listed under it with its own cost. Copy, switch off and delete (asked twice) too.
- Inventory items get a yield (the usable share after trimming), the units they are usually used and counted in, and their own units (1 slice = 20 g); a unit a recipe uses can't be deleted.
- Two new access areas: Recipes (read recipes without costs, or change them) and Costing (see costs). Owners and admins have both, operators read recipes, viewers neither.
- Reports: Recipes, Recipe lines and Recipe costs (by venue), with the starters Recipes not fully costed and Recipe cost by venue. Pepper answers "what does the cheeseburger cost at Leeds?" and "what goes into the cheese sauce?".
