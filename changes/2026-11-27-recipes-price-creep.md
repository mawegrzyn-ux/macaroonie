# Price creep: the dishes a supplier price rise took over their cost target

- Every time a supplier price changes in the app (a price added or edited, its pack size, a venue's own price, an inventory import, a supplier's price list by product code, an invoice's prices applied after a delivery), the dishes whose recipes use that item are checked at each venue, also through prep recipes (a stock inside a soup) and the meal deals that hold them. A dish that was on target and is now over it, or over its target and now over its limit, is a cost alert. A trial run of an import changes nothing.
- Menu margins shows a banner when dishes crossed and nobody has looked yet: "4 dishes crossed 35% since 1 Dec: Chicken thighs up 12%". See them lists the last 90 days: each dish's cost % before and after, the % it crossed, which ingredients moved and what that did to a serving, where the change came from. Mark as seen (Costing manage) clears it for everyone and keeps who saw it.
- Today's "dishes over the cost target" card adds the same line, opens the list, and comes back when another dish crosses.
- A rule such as "when a dish goes over 35%, tell the owner" now tells at the moment of the price change instead of within the hour, still once per crossing.
- Ask Pepper "why did the chicken platter's cost go up since 1 November?": it compares what a serving costs now with what the same recipe cost at that day's supplier prices, ingredient by ingredient.
- Reports: a Cost alerts subject (ask for price rises or cost creep) and the starter Dishes that crossed the cost limit. The Supplier price changes report shows each row's pack size.
- The price history now keeps the pack size with each price, so a cost per kilo can be read back for any day.
- Migration 315 (cost alerts, the pack size in the price history).
