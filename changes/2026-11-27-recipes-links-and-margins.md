# Dishes linked to recipes by venue, revenue stream and order type, and menu margins

- Every dish on your menus can be linked to its recipe: the Recipes page has a Dishes tab listing each dish with the recipe it uses, how many places use another one, and whether it can be costed. Dishes with no recipe are matched to recipes by product code, then name ("Link 14 dishes to their recipes", checked in a list before saving), and retail goods whose barcode is a stock item's get a one-line recipe of it ("Link 6 goods to their stock items").
- The recipe matrix: open a dish to see, for every venue and each of its revenue streams by each order type, which recipe it uses there and what a portion costs. Set a takeaway version for Take out, a venue's own version for Soho, or two portions for a double; the closest setting wins, the same way a dish's VAT is decided. Set cells are solid and can be cleared (asked twice); inherited ones say which cell they follow. On a phone it is a list per venue.
- Options change the recipe: extra cheese adds 30 g of cheddar, oat milk swaps the milk, large is the recipe times 1.5, a side of fries is the fries recipe. The Options tab lists every option with its rule, and the ones that still need one; a dish can have its own rule for an option. The till's No, Light and Extra act on the option's own ingredient.
- Menu margins (Stock, after Recipes): every dish on a menu at a venue, revenue stream and order type, live or draft, with its price, the price net of VAT, its cost, gross profit and cost %, coloured against your targets (30% and 35% to start; a menu section can have its own). Options that cost extra show their own margin, meal deals show the range by what is picked (each end over what those picks sell for, extra charges included, coloured by the highest), and dishes that can't be costed are listed first with how to fix each. Tap a dish to see why; Export CSV.
- The recipe page shows the menu dishes it is used on, with Link a dish. Copying a menu keeps its dishes' links; links to a dish that has gone from every menu are removed after 30 days.
- Reports: Recipe links and Dish margins, with the starters Dishes over the target cost % and Dishes with no recipe. Pepper answers "what does the burger cost to take away at Soho?" and "which dishes on the lunch menu are over target?".
- Migrations 302 (recipe links, option rules, cost targets) and 303 (the Menu margins menu link).
