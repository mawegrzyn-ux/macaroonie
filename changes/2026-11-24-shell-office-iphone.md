# Macaroonie Office is ready to be built for the iPhone

- The app's iPhone settings are in place (the reasons it gives for the camera, microphone, speech recognition and photos; push for iPhones), and what works differently on an iPhone (the keyboard, speech, barcode scans) is handled.
- It still needs the owner's Apple developer account before the first iPhone build; the steps are in office-app/README.md.
- Notices sent to the app now say which phone they go to (on iPhone, on Android) in your Notices list.
