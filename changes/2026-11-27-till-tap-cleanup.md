# Till button tap counts are deleted after 90 days at every venue

- The counts of the buttons each person taps on a till (the layout designer's Taps heat map and the till activity reports) are now deleted after 90 days at every venue, every hour, also at a venue whose tills no longer send any. Before, a venue's old counts went only when one of its tills next sent new ones.
- Help → Till → Which buttons get used: tell your staff the tills count their taps; the Privacy policy describes it.
