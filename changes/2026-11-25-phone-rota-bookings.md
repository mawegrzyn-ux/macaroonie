# The rota and bookings on the phone

- Rota tile: this week's rota on the phone, arrows or a swipe for the week before or after. By day shows who's on each day with their shifts, role, who's sick or on holiday and the day's hours; By person shows each person's week in one row; Me shows your own shifts.
- Managers tap a person's day to set each shift on, off, sick or holiday, and Save. It is the same rota as the office.
- Timeline tile: today's bookings table by table, the times scrolling sideways, a red line at now. Tap a booking for its details and to mark it arrived, seated, no-show or cancelled (cancel and no-show ask twice).
- Guest list tile: the day's bookings by time with covers, table, status and notes, a warning sign when the notes mention an allergy, search by name or phone, filters by status, and Arrived / Seated on each row. The tile shows how many bookings are on today.
- The + on both pages makes a booking: restaurant, day, covers, then the times still free, then the guest's name, phone, email and notes.
- The office's booking drawer and Bookings list now offer the same statuses as each other: Arrived only where the restaurant uses it.
