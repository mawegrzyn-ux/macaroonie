# Stock: sales taken off by their recipes, the Usage tab, dish margins as sold, and ordering up to par

- Stock on hand now takes sales off. Every few minutes Macaroonie works out what each dish sold used: on the till and the kiosks (with each line's options and a meal deal's picks), online orders (options by their names, collected or delivered as your order types say), and another till's receipts through the Item mapping (a meal deal from there at the dishes guests pick most in it). Each dish uses the recipe it uses there: the takeaway version for a takeaway, a venue's own where it has one. Test till sales are left out, and so are voids (one marked wasted is already in Waste).
- Today's sales are taken off up to the last run: the On hand tab says when (up to the last run at 14:05). An item's history shows each day of sales as one line with how much went through the till, online and other tills, and On hand shows what was sold since the count.
- A new Usage tab on the Stock page: what sales used by item or by day with each channel's part and, with Costing view, its value; the dishes sold without a full recipe with why and how to fix each (no recipe, an option with no rule, an online option renamed since, a unit with no size, a recipe Pepper read in that nobody checked); and Recompute (Stock manage) to work days out again now.
- Late sales (a till that syncs the next morning, an order cancelled, another till's lines mapped) are picked up by themselves for the last two weeks, and saving a recipe, a dish's link or an option rule works those days out again with it.
- Refunds on Loyverse or Square give nothing back to stock unless Refunds on other tills give stock back is switched on in Stock settings.
- Menu margins has a Sold tab: what really sold at a venue over the days you pick, by dish and section, with units, net sales, theoretical cost, GP and cost %. Net sales, GP and cost % need till reports view as well; a dish none of whose items has a price shows no cost rather than £0.00.
- A dish taken off the menus after it sold still takes its recipe off stock when its day is worked out again.
- Ordering up to par: once a venue has an approved count, Fill to par and the Par button order the par less what is on hand and what is already on order, and each line of the order form shows on hand, on order and what sales used in the last 7 days. Each venue can choose Order up to par or Order the par in Stock settings. Up to par needs Stock view: without it the order form (and Pepper) orders the par as it is, and says so.
- A meal deal on Menu margins has a typical cost: at the dishes guests pick most.
- Pepper: "how much chicken did we sell through yesterday?" and "why isn't the soup taking anything off stock?" (usage_summary); stock on hand says sales are taken off; Fill to par from Pepper orders up to par too.
- Reports: Usage from sales (each item each trading day, by channel) and Dish sales and theoretical cost, with the starter Theoretical food cost by week; Uncosted dishes by sales now reads what was really sold.
- Migration 311 (usage by day, dish sales by day, each day's fingerprint, each venue's run; the refunds and par settings).
