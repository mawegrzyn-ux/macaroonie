# Menu engineering from what really sold, what-if scenarios, and pushing new prices into a menu's draft

- Menu margins has a new Engineering tab: each menu section's dishes as stars (sell well and earn at least the section's average gross profit per sale), plough-horses (sell well, earn less), puzzles (earn more, sell little) and dogs (neither), from what really sold at a venue or every venue over the days you pick. A four-box chart per section (tap a dish for its figures and what to do), a table per section with units, share, GP per unit, total GP and cost %, and one line on what to do with each kind.
- A meal deal rung up as a deal counts as one dish of its own section. An automatic deal that the till, a kiosk or online ordering finds among dishes rung up one by one is a discount over those dishes: they count in their own sections at what they took after the saving, and the tab names the menu's automatic deals. Units that couldn't be costed count for popularity but never for gross profit; a section with none costed has no average (a dash, never £0.00). GP, cost % and the kinds need Till reports view as well as Costing view.
- A new Scenarios tab: try price and cost changes on a menu (every dish, a section or one dish; up or down by a percentage or a figure, or set; prices rounded to 1p, 5p, 10p, 50p or £1) and see the cost % weighted by what really sold and the gross profit at those units, before and after, dish by dish. Save, reopen, copy and delete scenarios (Costing manage to save), and export them to CSV.
- Push prices writes a saved scenario's new prices into the menu's draft, after a preview of every price that will change and where the menu is sold. It needs Menus manage as well as Costing manage, and never publishes: the new prices go live only when the menu is published on the Menus page.
- Pepper: "which dishes are dogs on the lunch menu?" (menu_engineering) and "save a scenario with every price up 5%" (save_scenario, shown on a card before it saves). Changing a saved scenario keeps its venue, order type, days and notes unless you name new ones, and the card says what else changes.
- Reports: Menu engineering and Cost scenarios, with the starter Menu engineering this month.
- Migration 314 (cost scenarios).
