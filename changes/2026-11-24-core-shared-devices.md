# A kitchen tablet or a pass phone can be shared: each change is made as the person who typed their PIN

- An owner or admin marks a device as shared for a venue (Settings, or This device on the phone pages). On it, anyone can look things up, and before any change it asks Who is this?: tap your name, type your PIN.
- A till PIN is enough for logging checks (fridge, hold, cooking and delivery checks, checklist ticks, corrective actions, and Pepper's cards for them). Anything else needs your own office PIN, which you set yourself in Settings (or This device) on your own phone or computer.
- With an office PIN you can do what your own role may do there, never more than the account signed in on the device.
- The food safety log, the checklist, the H&S action log, the task history and Pepper's Done list name the person, not the account.
- Lock is one tap on the pill at the bottom right, and the device locks itself after 2 minutes without a tap. Five wrong PINs lock that person on that device for 5 minutes.
- Migration 280 (shared devices, office PINs, who is on each device, wrong PINs, and each change made on a shared device).
