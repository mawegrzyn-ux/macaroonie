# Stock: variance by period, actual and theoretical food cost, closing a period, and why an item is out

- A new Variance tab on the Stock page puts a pound figure on the gap between what the counts say was used and what the dishes sold should have used. It works by period: from one approved full count at a venue to the next, made when the later count is approved, with the period since the last full count running to now.
- For every item: actual usage (opening count, plus what was received and sent in from another venue, less what was sent away and the closing count), what sales took by their recipes, waste (the till's voids marked wasted too), adjustments, the variance and the part nothing explains. Items are listed by pounds of unexplained loss, biggest first, with each one's share of the venue's food sales, and the ones over the band are flagged.
- Food cost: the actual food cost % beside the theoretical, the gap in points, and whether the venue's unexplained loss is over its band. Net food sales are food and drink, VAT out; retail goods and services are left out. Pounds need Costing view, the food cost % and shares of sales Till reports view as well.
- Tap an item for why it may be: dishes sold without a recipe that may use it, deliveries changed after the count, on hand going below zero, a unit that changed, waste, adjustments, transfers not received, late sales, remakes; each with where to put it right. Its figures between each of its counts show too.
- By venue shows one item at every venue (which site loses most on chicken); Key items shows what each key-item count found since the one before, so a daily count gives daily figures.
- Closing a period (Stock manage) freezes its figures like a Z; its days are never worked out again, and a sale that reaches one late counts in the next period, marked. Reopening needs a note, works the days out again with the late sales in them, and waits until any later closed period is reopened first.
- A closed period with flagged items nobody has reviewed shows on Today as Variance to review, for Stock manage, until each is marked as reviewed on the item.
- Stock settings has the Variance band: points of food sales for a venue (1 by default), and the item rule (over 3% of what sales took and over £50).
- Pepper: "what was our food cost last week against theoretical?" (variance_summary) and "why are we short on chicken?" (explain_variance).
- Reports: Stock variance by period, Food cost by period and Stock periods, with the starters Variance by item and Theoretical against actual food cost by week.
- Migration 312 (stock periods, reviewed flags, late sales on closed days, the variance band, a sold dish's type).
