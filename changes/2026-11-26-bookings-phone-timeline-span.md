# Phone timeline: a booking on several tables is one box

- On the phone's Timeline a booking on several tables is now one box across those tables' rows, with the name, time, covers and the tables ("T4 + T5"), instead of a separate block on every table.
- That is for tables next to each other in the list (the order set on the Tables page). Where a booking's tables are apart it is one box per group of neighbouring tables, each with a dashed edge, a link mark and the other tables ("also T8"); tapping any of them opens the same booking.
- Other bookings on those tables at other times stay in their own rows, as before. A short booking's box is now at least 48 px wide.
- The Guest list and a booking's sheet name the tables of a booking on several ("T4 + T5") rather than the combination's own name.
