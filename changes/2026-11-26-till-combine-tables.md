# Combine tables on the till

- A party that sits across several tables is one order. On the floor, tap Combine tables, tap the party's tables, then Combine: free tables open one order holding them all, and a table with an open order takes the others. It works on the plan, on a phone's tiles and on a phone's rows.
- A table that has an order of its own is merged in after one question ("Table 5 has an open order with 3 items (£24.50). Combine and merge it into Table 4's order?"). Merging needs Move items between checks, or a manager's PIN.
- From an order: More, Combine tables (also in Move table, and as a till layout button) to join more tables or split one off.
- The party's tables show as one on the floor: the same colour, a ring, a link mark saying "4 + 5", a line between them on the plan, and the total, guests and timer once. Tap any of them to open the order. The lists show one entry, "Tables 4 + 5".
- Kitchen tickets and screens, bills and receipts, the closed checks list, Till reports and the report builder (the new Tables and Tables held fields) name every table.
- Paying, cancelling or merging the order frees every table. Moving the party keeps its joined tables.
- When the first table is in one of your booking combinations (Tables, Combinations), the combination is offered as a one-tap pick.
- Migration 297 (pos_checks.joined_table_ids).
