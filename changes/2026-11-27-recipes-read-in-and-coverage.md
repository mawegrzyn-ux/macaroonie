# Recipes read in by Pepper, recipes for dishes, and how much of what you sell is costed

- Read recipes (Recipes page, for people who change recipes): Pepper reads photos of recipe cards (take one on a phone or tablet), a PDF, a spreadsheet (Excel or CSV) or text pasted in, many recipes at once. Each line is matched to your inventory items and prep recipes (by Pepper, the item's code or its name) and shown as matched, suggested or not found with the words read. Accept a suggestion, pick an item, add a missing one to the inventory in place, leave a line out or untick a recipe, then make the drafts in one go. A sauce read in the same file as the burger that uses it is linked to it.
- Recipes Pepper wrote are drafts, marked "Draft, not checked": costed but shown as partly costed, and so is every dish linked to them, until someone checks each one and makes it live. Lines nobody matched are kept in the recipe's notes, never guessed. Drafts you make yourself (copies, variations) are costed as before.
- Make a recipe for this dish (the Dishes tab and a dish's matrix): the recipe starts with the dish's name, product code and section, is linked to the dish for every venue, and the dish's options are listed with what each does.
- The Recipes page opens on "Costed: 62% of the last 30 days' sales" for a venue or every venue, by till, kiosk, online and other tills (test till sales left out), with the dishes not costed yet, best sellers first, each with how to fix it and a button to where it is fixed.
- Units and yield (an inventory item): Suggest a typical yield asks Pepper what is usually usable once trimmed (a whole salmon about 55 to 60%); it is only used when you tap Use and saved with Save.
- Pepper reads recipes in from the chat (attached photos, PDF or CSV, or pasted), writes a draft recipe from a description, links dishes to recipes for every venue or at a venue, revenue stream or order type (with Undo), and says how much of what sold is costed. Each change is shown on a card first. "New recipe" is a form Pepper can open.
- Reports: Recipe coverage (each dish sold on a day, costed or not, and why) and the starter Uncosted dishes by sales.
- Migration 304 (where a recipe came from: read in or made by Pepper).
