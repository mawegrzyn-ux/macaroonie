# For review: what waits for you, and Pepper works with today's tasks

- New page For review (in the menu after Pepper; Home shows how many things wait). Waiting lists Pepper's cards you didn't answer, supplier orders waiting for your approval and invoices Pepper read for a delivery that isn't booked in yet, each with where it came from and Approve, Edit and Reject. The second tab is Done by Pepper.
- Pepper's cards no longer vanish after 30 minutes: a card you leave waits up to 7 days and can be approved later, the same as pressing Confirm (a table held for a guest still runs out on its own time).
- Several cards of one kind that can be undone (say, sold out dishes) can be approved together. Nothing about money, pay, allergens or a deletion ever is.
- Pepper reads your task list ("what's on my list?", and Brief me on today now names the late tasks), and can snooze a task, skip one for the day (always with your reason) or give one to someone, each on a card you confirm. A snooze can be undone.
- Migration 279 (For review in existing menus).
