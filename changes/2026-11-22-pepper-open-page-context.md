# Pepper's page links open where you asked, and Pepper finds pages like the menu search

- "Open the rota for Ware next week" gives a link that opens the Rota at Ware on next week; a day, a week and a tab work the same way on the Timeline, Bookings, Food safety, Online orders and Cash recon. Pepper says when a page doesn't open at what you asked.
- Pepper's page finder uses the menu search's own matching (names, search words, descriptions), so the two match pages the same way; the extra words Pepper kept of its own are now the pages' search words, which the menu search box finds too: "cashing up" finds Cash recon, "hot holding" Food safety, "christmas" Schedule.
