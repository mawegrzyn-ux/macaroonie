# Menus or catalogues

- A menu that sells retail goods or services is called a Catalogue: the Menus page marks it, and Pepper calls it that.
- The Menus page and the Menus link in the main menu say Menus, Catalogues, or Menus & catalogues, from what you sell. Pick the word yourself in Call them on the Menus page. A link you renamed in the Navigation designer keeps your name.
- Migration 269 (`tenants.catalogue_word`).
