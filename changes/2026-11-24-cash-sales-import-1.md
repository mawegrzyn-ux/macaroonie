# Sales from other tills, phase 1: every Loyverse receipt kept in Macaroonie

- A new **Integrations** page (Money > Integrations, after Cash recon) lists the other tills whose sales Macaroonie keeps. Connect a Loyverse account there (paste an access token), link each venue to its Loyverse store with its trading-day start, and see each connection's status (Active, Syncing, Late, Paused, Needs attention), its last sync, its webhook and, per venue, the history import's progress and how many receipts, refunds and cancellations are kept, from which day to which.
- Every Loyverse receipt is kept with its lines, variants, modifiers (under their item), discounts, taxes (included or added), tips, surcharges and payments, in pence exactly as Loyverse recorded it. Refunds link to the sale they refund; a cancelled receipt is marked cancelled. Customers are kept as Loyverse's customer number only, never a name, email or phone. The master data (items, variants, modifiers, categories, taxes, discounts, payment types, stores, staff, devices) is kept too, ready for the item mapping that comes next.
- Receipts arrive within a minute or two through a webhook registered with Loyverse when you connect (or an address to add in Loyverse yourself), and every 15 minutes anyway. Linking a venue fetches its last 13 months in the background; Fetch history starts it again, Sync now checks straight away, Pause stops a connection, Disconnect removes it and its kept sales (it asks first).
- The Loyverse account and store link moved from Cash Recon settings to Integrations; the Loyverse tab in Cash Recon settings keeps the field mapping, with a link to Integrations. Fill from Loyverse works as before.
- Migrations 288 (the Loyverse tables renamed and made general: pos_connections, pos_connection_venues), 289 (ext_documents, the raw copies), 290 (ext_checks and its lines, payments, discounts and taxes; ext_catalog) and 291 (the Integrations link in saved menus).
