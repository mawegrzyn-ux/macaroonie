# The Launcher has gone

- The Launcher (a screen of tiles that could replace the whole menu for a group) has gone, with its switch on the Navigation page and the "Show as a quick-access tile" box on each menu entry. The sidebar is the only menu.
- Your own shortcuts are your pins on Home. In a group that used the Launcher, everyone who had no pins yet finds the Launcher's tiles as their pins, in the same order.
- Migration 274 (drops `tenants.nav_style`, `nav_items.show_in_launcher` and `launcher_sort_order`).
