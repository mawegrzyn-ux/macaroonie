# Stock: waste, dishes voided on the till as wasted, and transfers between venues

- Waste: the Stock page has a Waste tab, and the phone a Waste tile. Log an item (a tray of chicken), a prep batch (half a kilo of curry paste) or a dish (2 burgers) with how much and why; before you save it shows what it takes off stock, a batch or a dish broken into its ingredients by its recipe (a dish taken away can have a recipe of its own), and what it was worth for people who see costs. When the recipe can't say (no recipe, a unit with no size, a recipe Pepper read in that nobody checked) the line says so. A photo can go with it, taken with the phone's camera in the Office app.
- Each day's waste is listed with its reason, kind, who and when, with the day's total by kind and the last seven days by reason, kind, item or person. Undo takes an entry back on the same trading day (whoever logged it, or Stock manage); after that day Stock manage reverses it, dated with the original so a count since keeps its figure.
- Waste reasons are the group's, each one of WRAP's three kinds: spoilage, preparation and plate. Every group starts with nine; add, change, switch off or drag them on the Stock page's Settings tab. A reason that has been used is switched off rather than deleted.
- A dish voided on the till and marked as wasted is waste by itself: it shows on the Waste tab and in reports with the void's reason and check, broken into its ingredients and taken off stock, and is never entered again. Test sales are left out.
- Transfers: send stock from one venue to another on the Transfers tab. It leaves the sender at once; the other venue receives it (it shows on their Today as a transfer to receive) and says what actually arrived: what didn't is kept as lost in transit. A transfer on its way can be cancelled at the venue that sent it, and the stock comes back. Valued at the sending venue's average cost. On a shared tablet a person sends from, and receives at, the tablet's own venue.
- On hand and each item's history now take waste and transfers in.
- Pepper: "we dropped a tray of 12 chicken thighs" logs the waste with its reason (Undo takes it back), "what did we waste this week?" adds it up, and it can send and receive transfers, each confirmed first. The forms Log waste and Send stock to another venue open from Pepper too.
- Reports: Waste (each item of each entry, till voids included) and Stock transfers (sent, received, lost in transit), with the starters Waste by reason this week and Transfers in transit.
- Migrations 308 (waste reasons, waste and the items each took off) and 309 (transfers and their lines).
- Review fixes (migration 310): what a dish voided on the till as wasted took off is kept within minutes, so taking the dish off every menu or changing its recipe later no longer takes past waste out of stock or the Waste report; waste photos must be real photos; the Stock page's Waste and Transfers tabs open from Pepper and menu search finds them; a shared tablet logs waste only for its own venue; a reversed mistake no longer counts as two entries; a Waste report row opens the Waste tab on its day; waste reasons are switched off with Save.
