# Retail goods and services left out of allergens

- The allergen matrix and the Allergens page don't list retail goods or services.
- The till, kiosks, the website, online ordering and printed menus show no allergens, diet tags or calories for them; a kiosk no longer says "No allergens listed" on a mug.
- Pepper doesn't offer them as answers to allergy questions.
- The tags already set on them are kept in the menu, so switching an item back to food brings them back.
