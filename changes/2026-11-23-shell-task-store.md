# Today's tasks can be assigned, snoozed and skipped, late ones reach the managers, and six more kinds of task

- Every task on Home (and the phone's Today page) has a ... button: Assign it to someone whose role can do it, Snooze it for 30 minutes to 4 hours or until a time today, or Skip it for the day. A skip always needs a reason: pick one or type your own. Each is saved on its own.
- What was snoozed, skipped or marked done shows under the tasks in Off today's list, with the reason or the time it comes back, and Bring back.
- Managers see Skipped today on Home: the day's skips and their reasons at each of their sites, never who skipped them.
- A task still late two hours on also goes to the venue's managers as a notice (if they have late notices on). Snoozed and skipped tasks send nothing.
- Six more kinds of task: a booking email that failed or bounced today, reviews waiting to be checked, a menu screen or till that stopped answering while the venue is open, sold out dishes, and next week's rota still empty from Thursday.
- New report subjects Today tasks by site and Today task changes (Reports, Your group): what was finished, finished in time, went late or was skipped, venue by venue.
- Migration 278 (the task store, its history and the day's record of the list).
