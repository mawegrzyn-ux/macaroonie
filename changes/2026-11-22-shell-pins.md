# Your pins, kept with your login

- Pins are your own shortcuts to the pages you open most. Tap **Pin a page** (or **Edit pins**), add pages from your own menu, drag them into order by the grip, and tap **Save pins**. Up to 12.
- A pin can open its page at one venue, and on a page with tabs at one tab (Rota at Ware, Food safety on its Holds tab), and can have a name of your own.
- Pins are kept with your login, not in the browser, so the same pins show on every device you sign in on. Nobody else sees them.
- They replace the Overview's Quick access shortcuts, which were kept in each browser: the Overview's tile is now **Your pins**. Shortcuts picked before are not carried over.
