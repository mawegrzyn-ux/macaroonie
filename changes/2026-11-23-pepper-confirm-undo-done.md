# Pepper's cards show before and after, take a typed word for big changes, offer Undo, and every change is listed in Done by Pepper

- Pepper's Confirm cards now show the record as it is now beside how it will be (a dish going from on to sold out, a booking from 19:00 to 20:00, a guest's phone number), so you can check the change at a glance.
- On some cards you can fix a detail before confirming: press Change and correct the time, party size, reading, amount or note. It is checked again with the same rules before anything is saved.
- Changes that can't be put back easily (anything deleted, a guest's, customer's or staff member's details, pay, tips and wages, allergen information) need you to type a word the card shows, such as the guest's surname or CONFIRM, before Confirm works. These can never be confirmed by voice.
- Pressing Confirm twice, on two devices or after the connection drops now makes the change once and shows how it went the first time.
- Small changes that can be put back (sold out, pausing online orders, the website banner, ticking a checklist, a local event, a supplier out of stock) show Undo on the card for 10 minutes. Undo puts things back as they were, and only the person who confirmed the change can undo it.
- New page Done by Pepper (in the menu after Pepper): what Pepper changed, when, at which venue, how it was confirmed, the before and after, and how it went, with Undo while it is still open. By venue and day; your own changes, or everyone's for an owner or anyone who manages Pepper. It never ranks or counts people.
