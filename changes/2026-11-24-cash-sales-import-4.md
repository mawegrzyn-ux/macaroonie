# Sales from other tills, phase 4: Loyverse venues reported like your own tills

- **Fill from Loyverse uses the stored receipts.** Cash Recon no longer asks Loyverse when you fill a day: it reads the receipts the Integrations page has already brought in, so the window opens quickly and its figures match reports. The top of the window says how far the sync has reached.
- **A day that hasn't synced yet says so.** Instead of filling zeros, the day shows Not synced yet and can't be ticked; tap Sync now, wait a minute and Read again. A receipt that reaches Loyverse late is picked up by the next sync: fill the day again to take it in.
- **Reports across tills.** Three new subjects under Till sales put your own till and the venue's Loyverse till side by side, with a Till column: Sales, every till; Dishes sold, every till (Loyverse items as your dishes where they are mapped, the rest under the name they were sold as, a category counted under your section); and Payments, every till. Sales count the way your own till counts them, refunds apart. The Till sales subjects you had stay your own till only.
- **Mapping coverage in reports**: a figure on Dishes sold, every till, the share of the other till's sales that is mapped, kept as sold or counted under a section.
- **Home** shows the day's sales by till in the day at a glance (for people who can see Till reports).
- **Pepper**: "what did we take yesterday?" at a Loyverse venue is answered from the stored receipts, saying where the figures came from.
- No migration.
