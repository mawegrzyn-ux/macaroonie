# Starter reports for sales on every till, Loyverse included

- Six new starter reports under Till sales show your own till and another till linked on the Integrations page (Loyverse) together: Sales by day, every till; Sales by till (your till against Loyverse, with each one's share); Dishes sold, every till (Loyverse items as your dishes where the item mapping has them); Sales by section, every till; Payments by tender, every till; and Items still to map (Loyverse items the mapping doesn't place yet, most sold first).
- The Till sales starters you had before (Sales by day, Sales by dish and the rest) are still your own till only; Help, Integrations says which is which.
