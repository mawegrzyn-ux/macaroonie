# The phone app tidied up

- The phone app is called Office: in its top bar, and as the name it installs under from ops.macaroonie.com.
- The home screen starts straight with Capture and the tiles; the Add to home screen line is gone.
- One three-dot menu at the right of the top bar holds what used to float or sit as separate buttons: who is signed in, the shared device (who said who they are, Lock, Switch person, or Who is this?), This device, Switch location, Notices and Sign out. There is no floating "who is this?" chip on the phone any more.
- The Wages, Pepper, Notices and This device tiles are gone from the home screen: Wages still opens from Today's wages task, Pepper from the ask bar at the bottom, Notices and This device from the menu.
- Expenses: a receipt photo you take or pick shows the full width of the sheet, big enough to read, with Retake and Remove.
- Stop sharing a device works for the owner on the shared device itself. Owners, admins and the person who shared a device can stop it; on the device, someone with an office PIN types it first, and without one the device signs out once it stops, so getting back in takes your own sign-in.
- A supplier order's page (on the phone and in the office) opens with a plain card: the delivery day, the status in big letters and the supplier's reference beside it. The three dots on the card hold Print, PDF, Excel, Copy for checkout, Codes for quick order, Correct the delivery and Details and history (who asked for it, placed, sent and received it, and when).
