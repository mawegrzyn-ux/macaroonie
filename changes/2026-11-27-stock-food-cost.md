# Stock: food cost on Cash Recon, dashboards and Today; rules on cost, variance and waste

- Cash Recon's week has a Food cost card (below the grid, and under the wages in the card view): the week's theoretical food cost % (what its sales should have used by their recipes, over its net food sales, saying when dishes without a recipe, open items or dishes no longer on a menu make it read low) and the actual food cost from a stock period: the one that covers the whole week, said to be the period's figure, else the latest closed period, named with its dates. Actual needs a full count at both ends, so a week never gets one of its own. For people with Costing view and Till reports view.
- Dashboards: a Stock and costing group in Add widget with four widgets: food cost % actual and theoretical (the week's card and the latest closed periods), waste this week by reason, top variance items (the newest stock period's flagged items and biggest unexplained losses) and margin by menu (what sold in the week by menu: net sales, gross profit and cost %). Each shows what the person's access allows and says what is left out.
- The Overview has a Stock tile: each venue in a line with counts to approve, transfers to receive and flagged items to review, when it was last fully counted and, with Costing and Till reports view, this week's theoretical food cost and the latest actual.
- Today shows the dishes over their cost target at a venue (its live menus at today's supplier prices, each at the order type where it is worst), for people with Costing view, opening Menu margins. Home suggests what is missing in the set-up: no storage areas at a venue, best sellers with no recipe, and inventory items used in recipes with no supplier price, each to whoever manages it (whatever their Stock access), and nobody else sees the best sellers' names.
- Rules can start from three more things: a dish going over its cost target (looked for every hour, told once until it comes back under; a rule with its own %, "over 35%", tells as the dish passes that figure, a price rise after it went over included), a stock period closed with its variance over the band, and waste logged (over an amount). Pepper's make_rule reads them from a sentence. A cost or a share of sales is left out of a notice for people who can't see it, can't go into an H&S action, and making a rule on one needs Costing view.
- Migration 313: the new rule triggers, widgets and tile, and the dishes over target as the hourly sweep last found them.
