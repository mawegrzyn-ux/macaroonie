# Capture a photo, a voice note or a line on the phone for For review

- The phone's home screen and Today have a Capture button: take a photo (a delivery note, an invoice), say a voice note (you see and can change the words) or type a line, and it is kept with your name, the time and the venue.
- Nothing reads it straight away. It waits on For review, where Open in Pepper starts a new chat with it attached, and Dismiss takes it off the list.
- You see your own captures; people who manage Pepper see everyone's. On a shared device the capture is the person's who typed their PIN.
- Migration 281 (captures).
