# Speech knows your venue's own names

- When you talk to Pepper or record a voice note, near misses of your fridges, hold stations, dishes, staff and suppliers are put right: walking fridge becomes Walk-in fridge.
- Numbers are never changed, so a reading stays exactly as you said it, and a name heard right stays as it is.
- In the Macaroonie Office app the phone's own speech recognition is told your names before you speak.
