# Your own fields, and rules made from a sentence

24 Nov 2026, Pepper-first step 8B1 and 8B2 (migrations 285, 286)

- **Your own fields**: add fields of your own to customers, staff, suppliers and inventory items in Settings (text, a number, yes or no, a date, or one of a list). They show on each one's page with their own Save, on the new supplier and staff forms, in reports as fields of what the report is about, and Pepper fills them in by name.
- **Rules made from a sentence**: type "When a fridge reads above 8, add an H&S action for the manager" in Settings (or say it to Pepper), check the rule Pepper read on its card, and confirm. Rules start from a fridge reading, a supplier order placed, a task running late or a review, and can add an H&S action or send a notice to people's phones; never money, pay, prices, allergens, deletions or anything about a person. Each runs as the person who made it, a few times a day at most, listed under Done by Pepper.
