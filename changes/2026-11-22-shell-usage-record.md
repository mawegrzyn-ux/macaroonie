# A record of the pages each person opens and what they search the menu for

- The admin now notes, quietly in the background, which pages each person opens and what they type in Search the menu: whether they then handed the words to Pepper and what they opened (a menu link, a saved report, one of Pepper's picks, or nothing). Pages are kept by their address only, without anything after a ?, and with record numbers left out. Kept 90 days.
- Each person sees their own in Reports: **My pages and searches** shows only the person running it, an owner included.
- For the group, **Pages opened** (times each page was opened, by day) and **Menu searches** (searches, the share handed to Pepper, searches that opened something or found no menu match) carry no names and no words typed. They need View on Navigation.
- When a guest asks to be forgotten, menu searches that named them are deleted.
- Migration 270 (`usage_events`).
