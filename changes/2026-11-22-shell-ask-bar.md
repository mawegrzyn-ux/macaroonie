# The ask bar: find a page, or ask Pepper

- At the top of Home, one box finds a page or asks Pepper. As you type, the pages that fit show straight away (what each is for and where it sits in the menu), then saved reports whose name fits. Tap one to open it: no waiting for Pepper, and it works with Pepper switched off.
- The last row is always **Ask Pepper** with your words. Tap it, tap **Ask**, or press Enter without picking a row: Pepper opens at the side with a new chat that starts with your question. A few questions for the day sit under the bar on Home, one tap each.
- The same bar opens over any page: **Search or ask** at the top of the menu, the magnifying glass on the narrow menu, Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac), or / when you aren't typing in a box. It replaces the menu's own search box.
- When Pepper answers with a page, it shows a card: the page's name, where it sits in the menu, the venue, day or tab it opens at, and **Open**.
- Pepper's separate "picks" in the menu search are gone: the chat finds pages with the same search as the bar.
- No migration.
