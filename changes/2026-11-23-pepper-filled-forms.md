# Pepper fills in forms for you to check and save

- Ask Pepper to open a form and tell it what you know ("the Fresh Co delivery is in, two boxes short, 4 degrees"): it opens the form with those values already in, each marked Filled by Pepper. Change a value or press OK beside it; the form saves only when you press its own Save.
- Pepper only puts in what you said or what a photo or document you gave it says. It never guesses a reading: a temperature goes in only when you said the number yourself.
- On the fridge and hold grids and an order's quantities, Pepper's values aren't saved until you press OK or change them, and an order can't be placed while one is still waiting (OK all takes them all).
- After you save a form opened from the chat, its card shows a line of what was saved, with Edit to open it again. The line stays when you come back to the chat later.
- A delivery check, an expense, a corrective action, a new H&S action and a new booking keep what you've typed as a draft for you only, through a reload or on another device, until you save. Drafts left for a week are cleared.
- New forms Pepper can open: a supplier order, the day's corrective actions, a new H&S action, a new booking and a booking's details to change.
- When you give Pepper just one reading or one change ("the walk-in is reading 3", "book Jane for 4 at 7"), it now makes it straight away with a Confirm card instead of opening a form or asking first.
- Migration 275 (form drafts).
