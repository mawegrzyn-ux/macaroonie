# Stock: counts by storage area, approval, adjustments and each item's history

- A new Stock page (main menu, Stock, after Recipes): what is on hand at each venue, worked out from what happened (the last approved count plus every delivery and adjustment since), with what it is worth for people who see costs. Tap an item for its history: every delivery, count and adjustment with the balance after it.
- Storage areas per venue (Walk-in, Dry store, Bar), dragged into the order you walk round in, each with its items in shelf order. An item can be in two areas; the count adds them.
- Counting: a full count (every item; what nobody counted is confirmed as zero before it is submitted) or a key items count from a short list (proteins, cheese) that leaves the rest alone. Count area by area, in any of an item's units at once (2 cases + 3 trays + 400 g), on the office or on the phone in the walk-in with the Office app's scanner. Each line saves by itself as it is typed, and two people can count two areas at the same time.
- Approval: a submitted count shows what was expected against what was counted, with the value of each difference; on hand changes only when it is approved. Settings for a second person to approve (on a shared tablet, the person who switched in with their PIN is who counted or approved), and blind counts (expected hidden while counting, also on Pepper's count card). A count day per venue puts "Stock count due" on Today until that day's full or key-list count is submitted (a few items counted on their own don't clear it), and a submitted count shows to whoever may approve it; on the phone both open the count page.
- Adjustments for what fits nothing else, always with a reason, reversed rather than edited (a reversal is dated with what it reverses, so a count since then keeps its figure).
- A new access area, Stock: View sees stock, Use counts, Manage approves, adjusts and sets up. Owners and admins manage, operators count, viewers see. Custom roles took a level from what they had in purchasing.
- Pepper: "2 cases and 3 trays of chicken in the walk-in" records a count in those units; "what happened to the rice this week?" shows its history; it can start a key items count from a list.
- Reports: Stock on hand (moved from Purchasing), Stock moves, Stock counts, Count lines and Stock adjustments, with the starter Last count's differences.
- The Inventory page's Stock on hand tab now links to the Stock page. Sales still aren't taken off stock: that comes next with usage from sales; waste and transfers between venues come in the next step.
- Migrations 305 (the stock module), 306 (areas, key lists, settings, adjustments; counts with kinds, statuses and lines by area), 307 (the Stock menu link).
