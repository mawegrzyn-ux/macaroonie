# Barcodes and stock on hand

24 Nov 2026, Pepper-first step 8B3 (migration 287)

- **Barcodes**: inventory items and supplier prices take a barcode (the GTIN on the product, checked as you type). Search or scan it on the Inventory page and the order form; a supplier's barcode picks their price.
- **Stock on hand**: the Inventory page's Stock on hand tab shows each item at a venue as the last count plus what was delivered since, with a Count stock sheet (in kg, litres or each). Sales aren't taken off yet: dishes aren't linked to the items they use. The report Stock on hand shows the same.
