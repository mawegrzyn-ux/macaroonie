# The store status board as a honeycomb, full screen, and on a menu screen

- Store tech → Tech board opens as a honeycomb: a hexagon for each till, kitchen screen, kiosk, printer, card machine, menu screen and the venue hub, grouped by kind with how many are up ("Tills 2 of 3 up"). The fill says how it is (green working, amber needs a look, red down, grey not linked) with a mark for each (a tick, an exclamation mark, a cross, a dash); the ring says what kind of kit it is, each kind with its own colour and line, and a key under the board explains them. The big hexagon first is the venue overall ("1 down", "6 up, 1 down, 2 to check") with whether Macaroonie itself is up.
- Problems stand out: a down hexagon glows gently (not when your device asks for less movement), sits in the middle of its group and says when it was last seen; a printer with failed tickets today says how many; the venue hub's thin inner ring shows how up to date its copy for trading offline is. Needs attention lists what is down or needs a look, worst first.
- Tap a hexagon (or a line of Needs attention) for its details: its state, last seen, last activity, app version, a printer's waiting and failed tickets, a card machine's last payment, and a button to where it is set up. Escape or Close closes it.
- List shows the same kit as rows, now with a mark for each state; Dark or Light picks the honeycomb's look. Both are remembered on your device.
- Full screen puts the board on the whole screen, dark, for a back office TV or tablet: the venue's name and time, whether Macaroonie is up, the honeycomb as big as it fits and what needs attention, checked every 30 seconds. Escape or Close leaves it.
- Menu screens have a new block, Store status: the same honeycomb on a board, for the screen's own venue, checked about every 45 seconds. Pick which kit it shows, names on or off, the venue's health, what needs attention, the key, large or compact hexagons, and the words' size and colour; the designer shows example kit. It names the venue's kit, so it is meant for staff screens (back office, kitchen), and a screen only gets the kit when one of its boards has the block.
- Grey says which it is: "not linked" for kit not linked yet, "not checked" for linked kit with nothing to go on (a quiet card machine, a printer the hub hasn't checked). A printer on a venue hub that is down says Hub down. Only the hexagon itself takes a tap (not the corners of its box), the keyboard stays on a hexagon while the times move on, and full screen keeps the keyboard inside it and puts you back on the Full screen button when you leave.
- The Store status block shows the tills and other kit only when someone with Store tech access placed it or saved its board; placed by someone with Screens access only, it shows the menu screens.
- No migration.
