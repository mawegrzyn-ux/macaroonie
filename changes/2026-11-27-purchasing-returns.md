# Purchasing: returns to the supplier and their credit notes

- A delivery that was booked in now has a Returns card under its lines. A line booked in as damaged offers Return it, filled in (all that is left of it, Damaged, the delivery's note); Return items picks any lines that arrived, the packs going back (never more than arrived), why (damaged, wrong item, short dated, quality, other) and a note, and whether the goods go back now or went back with the delivery.
- What goes back comes off stock at the venue straight away: on hand says returned, the item's history shows the return, and variance takes it out of actual usage, so goods sent back aren't counted as loss. Cancelling a return puts the stock back as it was.
- Each return can be emailed to the supplier with a PDF (the lines, why, the price paid and the credit expected), or marked sent (handed to the driver, phoned, on their website); PDF and Print give the same document. When the credit note comes, record its number, date and amount (a different amount than expected is shown); Refused when the supplier won't credit it.
- The Purchasing page has a Returns tab beside Order and Deliveries: the credits still owed first, those waiting 14 days or more marked. Today shows "Chase the credit note from..." for a credit still owed two weeks after the goods went back.
- Who can do what: starting a return needs Purchasing at Use or more (a role can now be given Use on Purchasing to return goods without ordering); sending it, the credit note and refused need Purchasing approval. Money shows only to people who see purchasing prices.
- Pepper can start a return ("send back the two crushed cases of chicken from today's Brakes delivery"), confirmed first; sending it and the credit note stay on the page.
- Reports: Returns to suppliers and Return lines, the starter Credits still owed (by supplier), and returns in Stock moves and Variance.
