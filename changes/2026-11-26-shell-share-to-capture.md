# Share a picture to Capture, and From phone

- In the Macaroonie Office app on Android, a picture in your gallery (or any app) can be shared to Macaroonie Office: tap Share, choose Macaroonie Office, and the app opens on its home screen with Capture showing the picture. Add a line, check the venue and tap Keep for review.
- If you aren't signed in, sign in and Capture opens after (the picture waits ten minutes). Share several pictures and the first is kept, with a note saying so. A link or a note shared from another app opens as a typed capture. Anything else (a PDF, a video) is politely refused.
- Capture has a From phone button beside Photo: it picks a picture already on the phone (the gallery or its files), in the browser and in the app. The four choices (Photo, From phone, Voice note, Type) sit two by two.
- People whose role doesn't have Pepper are told the shared picture wasn't kept.
- Sharing needs a new build of the Macaroonie Office app; it isn't on the iPhone yet.
