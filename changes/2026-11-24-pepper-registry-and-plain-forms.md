# Pepper knows what can be done with things, and four set-up forms are drawn from the app's own rules

- Ask Pepper "what can I do with suppliers?", "can I delete a booking?" or "why can't I submit the cash-up?": it answers from the app's own list of what exists, with the changes it can make, the card each shows, the checks each makes, whether it can be undone, and the permission a change needs when your role hasn't got it.
- Adding a supplier, a staff member, an expense or closed dates is one simple form, the same from Pepper, from the Suppliers, Staff and Schedule pages and in the day's expenses. Its boxes and rules come from what the app accepts when you save; what Pepper put in is marked until you check it.
