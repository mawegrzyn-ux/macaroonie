# Allergen checks follow your changes, and dishes' calories are checked too

- A change that could alter what a dish holds now works its allergen check out again straight away: a supplier price, a product's allergens, the usual supplier at a venue, a supplier switched off, a substitute delivered, a recipe, which recipe a dish uses, or an option's rule. Only the dishes that use what changed are looked at.
- If that makes a new difference, the check opens (a dismissed or accepted one opens again, with its decision kept) and says what changed: "Oyster sauce: the usual supplier switched at Leeds". If nothing you decided changes, a dismissal holds.
- Today has a new card, Allergen checks to review, for whoever can decide them: it opens the Allergens tab on the checks a change opened (the Changed filter), and goes once they are accepted or dismissed.
- A product marked Substituted when a delivery is booked in counts at that venue for 14 days: unknown until what came is added to the price list with its allergens.
- Items have an energy figure on the Inventory page (kcal per 100 g, per 100 ml, or each). Each dish's check gains a Calories card: what its menu declares beside what one serving of its recipe comes to, through its prep recipes, with what gives the most and what isn't known. Accept writes the figure into the menu's draft; Dismiss keeps yours with a note. While any ingredient's energy is unknown there is no figure to accept.
- The recipe editor shows a recipe's own calories and what each dish using it declares.
- Calorie labelling on menus applies in England to businesses with 250 or more employees, and a franchise counts the whole network's employees: the Help says so, and to confirm the detail for your business.
- Reports: the subject Calorie checks, What changed on Allergen checks, the energy fields on Inventory items, and the starters Dishes with calorie differences and Items with no energy figure.
- Migrations 317 (what changed on allergen checks) and 318 (item energy, calorie checks).
