- Limits of what is built: a period, venues or other choice picked in the viewer lasts for the visit only, and the venues control is hidden for a group with one venue. Grouping by a link itself isn't offered; everything else shows only when the definition gives `limit.rest_as`; a filter naming a parameter the report doesn't have is dropped by `normaliseReport()` without a word; catalogue key aliases (plan 5.7) aren't built. Files: a list's file has no totals row; an Excel file is built in memory (200,000 cells; a longer list is cut with a note on its About sheet) and a CSV list holds 250,000 rows (its last line says so when cut). The kept results (a minute) are per API process, so another process reads again; the two running slots are database locks, shared by every process. Each person can keep 300 reports.
- Limits of the builder: no undo (leaving without saving goes back to the last save); filters made of several parts (`any`, `all`, `has`, which some starters use) are said in words and can be taken off, never made or edited on screen; tags aren't edited; the Data panel walks two links deep (a deeper path a starter uses still shows and runs, but can't be added from the panel); a top or bottom few ranks by a value that is shown; fields drag from the Data panel only when the three parts sit side by side (860 px of the page's own room), a tap works everywhere; the question before leaving covers the builder's own Back and View buttons and closing or reloading the tab, not the app's menu, the menu search or the browser's Back; Summary / List keeps what the other kind had only while the page is open; sharing needs Manage on Reports (someone with Use can't share their own report); Save as never carries a starter's key; the preview is the report as the person building it may see it (200 rows), so another role can see less; the values a text filter offers are the 60 most common in the report's period.
- Limits of drill-downs: the steps aren't in the address (a reload, or a link sent to someone, goes back to the report itself); a report can't name another report to open into; levels are the report's own list (the catalogue has no hierarchies), each field once on the way down, 8 at most; in a summary a tap means the row, and the figure tapped only where the report's figures count different rows; a figure with no rule of its own (a plain total, average or count of rows), or one reached through a list of other rows, lists every row behind the row; the row for everything else can't be opened, nor can a list; a share of the total in an opened row is its share of what is on the page; a figure compared with the period before opens this period's rows only; on a chart a bar, point or cell opens what its row opens, a legend entry or a figures tile doesn't. Tried in Chromium with a mouse and a touch context at 1280, 1015 and 390 px, not on a real tablet or phone.
- Limits of opening records: only a list's rows open a record (and the rows listed behind a summary's row); a summary's row, a chart's mark and a table across's cell open their drill-down as before. Pages there are: Till reports (a check, a trading day), Online orders (your own orders; GloriaFood orders have no page here, so in a list of online sales only some rows open), Bookings, Customers and the menu editor (a menu, a section, a dish). Staff, tables, revenue streams, order types, courses, promotions and venues have no page to open; a deposit opens its booking. A record opens on its own venue and day, so the page's own pick of venue and day changes with it. The bar back to the report is on the history entry: a reload keeps it, a new tab or a copied address doesn't (the record still opens), and it goes when another page is opened from the menu. The steps opened up and the period aren't in the report's address, so they come back only through the bar or the browser's Back. Rows don't open in files, on the print page, in the builder's preview or while a new result is coming. Tried in Chromium with a mouse and a touch context at 1280, 1015 and 390 px, not on a real tablet or phone.
- Limits of charts: not as the plan had them in three places, on purpose (say so when asked for them): no pie or ring (Parts of a whole is one bar cut into up to six parts with their shares, and sideways bars with shares beyond six; a copy of a starter made before 13 Nov 2026 that said `pie` reads as a table until a chart is picked again), never two scales on one plot (figures of different kinds get a plot each, one above the other, three at most), and an area of different values is drawn as lines until the report says to stack them and they add up.
