**16. Purchasing — follow-ups** *(Purchasing shipped with migration 153)*
- Approval is by role only (migration 180): no "orders over a set value need approval" rule, and approvers aren't told when a request comes in (no email or push; they see the count on the Order tab and in Pepper's day briefing).
- Splitting moves whole quantities between two orders of the same supplier; it can't send part of an order to another supplier, and a split PO that the supplier already had relies on them reading the Amended copy (nothing is phoned or confirmed).
- Stock counts: pars are a usual quantity; stock on hand exists since migration 287 (item 63) but "order to par" doesn't subtract what's on the shelf yet.
- Invoices and credit notes: no invoice matching against received quantities, no credit tracking for shortages beyond the line note.
- Costs: nothing feeds Cash Recon or a COGS / recipe costing view yet; `purchase_order_lines` hold the prices paid.
- `emailPo()` runs inside the request after the order is saved (one email per supplier); a slow provider makes Place order slow. Move to the notification queue if that shows up.
- PO files always include prices (they go to the supplier), so staff without price access can still see prices in a downloaded PDF/Excel.
- Not tried on a real tablet/phone (keypad temps, print from iPad).
- Delivery days (1 Nov 2026): a day placed as "agreed with the supplier" isn't recorded on the purchase order (no flag, note or name of who agreed). Bank holidays and a supplier's closed weeks aren't known: the day check only knows the weekly delivery days. (Per-venue lead days came with migration 233.)
- Supplier codes and the quick order (1 Nov 2026): the quick order is plain text and a CSV (nothing is sent to a supplier's website for you), and a supplier whose quick order box wants another layout (code and quantity in columns, a semicolon, the quantity first) gets ours. Codes are searched as typed: no fuzzy matching of a code mistyped.
- Catalogue tidy-up (1 Nov 2026): codes shared by two live prices before the rule stay until someone changes them (the check lists them; the price update leaves such a code out as on two products); there is no "merge two items" (add the other's prices to one and switch the other off by hand); the price update matches codes only (a price list without codes needs the import or the item panel), one supplier at a time, and never changes venue prices, packs or minimums; a collection price is ignored (no collection prices are kept); the name warning is advice only (two items with one name can still be saved).
- A supplier's basket (1 Nov 2026): codes only (a basket line without the supplier's product code, or a code not saved here, is left out; nothing matches by product name); one supplier per import; nothing is fetched from the supplier's website (the basket is pasted or exported by hand); the window takes files up to 2.9 MB and Pepper only CSV or text files attached in the chat (Excel through the page); a basket whose line is for an item already on the order on another price moves that line onto this supplier's price, never splits it in two.
- Out of stock (migration 234): one date per supplier price for every venue (not per depot or venue); no substitutes or one-click swaps (left for later: a substitute is usually another item); nobody is told when a price on their open order is marked out of stock (the order shows it next time it opens); no history of past out of stock periods (only the last mark, by whom and when); the date comes from staff, nothing reads it from the supplier.
- Delivery details and sent tracking (migration 233): the delivery slot is printed and copied, never checked against anything (the supplier's own slots aren't known); a PO's delivery phone, slot and account are the venue's settings as they are now, not stored on the order, so a change after ordering shows on the order's copies too. The duplicate guard only looks at the same supplier, venue and delivery day: a near-identical order for the next day, or a second supplier selling the same products, isn't flagged (the user's original 70% line match isn't built). Copy for checkout is plain text: nothing is typed into a supplier's website for you. A supplier's reference isn't checked for format or for being used twice. Today shows at most 10 tasks of each purchasing kind per venue.
- Deliveries without an order (migration 238): Pepper can't book one in (only shows them); a line added by someone without price access, as it came packed, has no price (counts £0 until a manager corrects it from an invoice); the setting is for the whole group, not per venue or supplier; the day can be up to 31 days back; nothing is checked against standing orders or a supplier's usual delivery days; the PO numbers come from the same sequence as orders; an invoice read for one that is never saved is kept (no clean-up).
- The purchasing screens' buttons are 44 px tall (`btn` in `components/purchasing/shared.jsx`, `h-11`), below the 48 px rule; the steppers are 48 px.
- Lines pinned before migration 232 took the venue's usual price at the time the migration ran, which may differ from what someone saw on screen before it (a pre-prod one-off).

**32. Invoices on receiving, receipts, product names — follow-ups** *(shipped with migration 197)*
- Tested only against a stand-in Claude; not yet with the live model and real invoices (a crumpled delivery note, a two-page PDF invoice, a handwritten one) or till receipts. First real use: book in a delivery from its invoice and compare every quantity and price; read a few receipts and check the VAT.
- Quantities are matched in the order's packs: when a supplier invoices in another unit (per kg against a case) the conversion is Pepper's (`qty_in_packs` / `price_per_pack`), not checked here beyond being a number. Pack sizes printed in words ("tray of 30") depend on Pepper reading them.
- One invoice per booking-in: a second invoice for the same delivery replaces the matches (both readings stay stored). Invoice lines matched to the same order line are summed; their prices aren't averaged (the first line's price is used).
- Charges (delivery, deposits, discounts) are shown, not recorded anywhere else; the order's delivery charge isn't compared with the invoice's. The invoice total isn't checked against the lines.
- Nothing yet uses invoice prices beyond the price list prompt (no credit notes, no invoice matching report, no cost reporting from received lines).
- Invoice files are stored like receipts (`/uploads/...`, public URLs on the local driver) and kept with the order; readings never applied are kept too (no clean-up).
- Receipt reading doesn't split one receipt into several expenses or read a receipt from a card statement; the category is picked only from the venue's own categories by exact name.

**63. Product codes and stock on hand, step 8B3 follow-ups (Pepper-first, 24 Nov 2026, migration 287)**

Built: a barcode (GTIN, check digit checked) on inventory items and supplier prices, found by the inventory search, the order form's search and the Office app's scanner; stock on hand per item per venue (last count plus what was received since), a count sheet on the Inventory page's Stock tab, and the report subject Stock on hand.
- **Sales aren't taken off**: no dish is linked to the items it uses (no recipes), so on hand is the count plus deliveries, and every place that shows it says so. Waste, transfers between venues and returns to the supplier aren't recorded either. `stockOnHand()` already takes `usage` for when recipes exist.
- **Barcodes aren't unique** (two items may carry the same one; a scan then lists both) and aren't on order documents. ~~Not in the CSV / Excel import or the price list update~~ Closed by step 9c (24 Nov 2026): a Barcode / GTIN / EAN / UPC column in both (a bad one listed with its row); the price list update's template file doesn't have the column yet, and the pasted-lines box takes no barcode. A barcode is only a GTIN: an own code stays the free-text item code. The till doesn't scan inventory items (they aren't sold).
- **Counts are per venue at a moment**: no count sheets by area or shelf, no par-based "order to par" using on hand yet (the Stock counts note above: pars don't subtract stock yet; since step 9b an item below its par is marked Below par on the Stock tab and found by Pepper's `stock_on_hand` with low), no variance report between counts, no undo or correction of a count (count again, also by Pepper's `count_stock`). A count's time can be up to 14 days back, not later.
- **What was received** is each booked-in line's received quantity in its packs at the order's booking-in time; a delivery corrected later changes the figure from then on. Lines whose item was deleted don't count.
- **The Stock tab is on the Inventory page**, which needs Purchasing setup; ordering staff with Purchasing only can read stock through the API and reports but have no page for it yet (the phone app has none).
- ~~Pepper has no stock tool; it sets barcodes on inventory items, not on supplier prices~~ Closed by step 9b (24 Nov 2026): `stock_on_hand` (items by name, code or barcode, or the low ones), `count_stock` (a card) and `barcode` on `add_supplier_price` / `update_supplier_price`. Left: `count_stock` takes the person's numbers as the card shows them, with no check against the words they said (as forms' measured values have); "low" needs a par on one of the venue's active order lists.
