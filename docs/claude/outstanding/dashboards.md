**38. Use level and dashboards per role — follow-ups** *(shipped with migration 229)*
- Use is offered on Checklists, Food safety logs and the H&S Action Log only. Other areas where a role may want the everyday work without the setup (Cash Recon days without settings, the rota without setup, online orders without ordering setup, purchasing ordering without the catalogue) still have only View / Manage, or their own split modules.
- Hiding a dashboard from a role tidies the tabs; it isn't access control for data (the widgets' own modules are). There's no per-person hiding, and a role made later sees every dashboard until someone unticks it.
- The built-in roles keep their defaults (Operator Manage, Viewer View on the H&S modules); nobody is moved to Use automatically.
