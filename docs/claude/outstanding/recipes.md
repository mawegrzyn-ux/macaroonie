**69. Recipes: what phase 1 left for later** *(phase 1 shipped 27 Nov 2026, migrations 298 to 301)*
- Built in phase 2 (27 Nov 2026): the links from dishes, options, meal deals and goods to recipes with the variation matrix, `margin()` in use on Menu margins, and the cost targets. Stock is still not taken off by sales (phase 5).
- Pepper can read recipes (`find_recipes`, `recipe`) but not change them: change tools (with Activity words in `shared/pepperActions.js` and a place in the registry) and the recipe reader come with phase 3.
- A unit a line uses is free text: a line can name a unit nobody has defined (it shows as a gap), and the editor's unit list offers only the units the item knows. There is no bulk "give these items a size" screen.
- The inventory import and price update don't read yield or units, and the item import doesn't set them; they are typed in the item's drawer.
- Last paid and average paid read received purchase order lines only; they don't follow stock moves, transfers or credit notes (phase 4 onwards), and the average is over a fixed 90 days.
- The Recipe costs report subject costs at standard prices only, today's; there is no history of what a recipe cost on a past day (D9: open days are recomputed, closed periods never change, comes with periods in phase 6) Periods came with phase 6a (27 Nov 2026): a closed period freezes its variance figures and its days' usage; recipe costs themselves still have no history.
- Recipe photos come from the media library through `PicturePick`, which needs the person to be able to upload to Media: a cook with Recipes manage but no media access sees the upload refused.
- No allergens, calories or labels from recipes (phase 8), and `shelf_life_hours` is kept but nothing prints a label with it. Allergens built 27 Nov 2026 (phase 8a, migration 316): item 80 lists what it left; calories are 8b, labels phase 10.
- Recipes are per group: nothing passes recipes from a franchisor to its franchisees (phase 10).
- Pepper's link to a recipe searches the Recipes page by its name (`?q=`) instead of opening the recipe: two recipes whose names contain the same words both show. Opening one straight from a name needs a link the page resolves by name.
- After an item's base unit changes, deliveries booked in under the old unit are left out of Last paid and Average paid; the gap then says nothing was booked in. It doesn't say the old deliveries were in another unit.
- This phase made the pepper area's `api/src/ai/tools/util.js` public in `areas.json` (every area's Pepper tool file uses it; four baseline crossings went with it). It is another area's public surface: name it in the pull request's title.

- The Dish margins report has a row for every live dish at each venue and active order type (no revenue stream): fine at today's sizes, heavier for many venues and big menus (one cost context per venue).

- Pepper's typical yield is a suggestion from the item's name; nothing records that a yield came from Pepper once saved.
- Pepper's `make_recipe` matches ingredients by an exact name or a sure match (`AUTO_MATCH`); anything else goes into the notes (it can't add inventory items itself: `add_inventory_item` can, as its own card).
- The plain form New recipe makes a recipe's details only (its lines are added on its page or by `make_recipe`).

**79. Recipes: what phase 7b (price creep, cost alerts, why_cost_changed) left for later** *(phase 7b shipped 27 Nov 2026, migration 315)*
- **What is a price change**: a supplier price added or edited (its price or its pack size), a venue's own price, the inventory import, a supplier's price list by code and an invoice's prices applied. A change of usual supplier, a price switched off or deleted, an item switched off, a recipe, link, option rule or menu price change, and the cost targets themselves are not: the hourly sweep sees those crossings (Today and the rules hear of them) and they make no cost alert. Last paid and average paid don't creep: alerts are at today's standard prices, as the Margins tab and the sweep judge.
- **Judged as the sweep judges**: each dish at its worst order type on the live menus at each venue (the group's menus at every venue), no revenue stream (a stream's own recipe isn't looked at) and without options (an extra's margin can creep unseen); a meal deal at its highest cost %. An alert is a crossing of the target or the limit; a rise within a band is no alert (a rule with its own % still hears of it). A price that falls back makes no "back under" alert: the alert stays until marked seen.
- **Before is the price history**: rows from before migration 315 have no pack (taken as the pack the price has now); a company price with no history row before the moment is taken as it is now (`unknown`, said by `why_cost_changed`). A venue's own price with no history row before the moment is read as no venue price then (the company price), which is right for one added since; one copied with a new venue by `createStore()` has no row until its first edit, so before that edit it reads as the company price too (the kitchen area's `storeSetup.js` could write a history row for each price it copies). Recipes keep no history, so `why_cost_changed` compares prices only, on today's recipe, and names the recipes changed since; menu prices aren't kept either, so its cost % then is at today's menu price.
- **The hook runs in the price change's transaction**: it loads every recipe and link of the group and the live menus (to find the dishes), then costs only those dishes at each venue and order type. A big import touching many items is slower by that; it takes the sweep's advisory lock, so a price saved while the hourly sweep runs for the group waits for it. A failure is logged and the price is still saved (no alert then; the sweep still sees the crossing within the hour).
- **Marking seen** is for the group (Costing manage): there is no seen per person, no snooze, and no note on an alert. The list shows the last 90 days; older alerts are in the report subject. Nothing deletes old alerts (they are small).
- **Pepper** reads (`why_cost_changed`) but can't mark alerts seen; there is no evaluation task for it yet. The phone has no price creep page (Today's card opens the office page).

**80. Recipes: what phase 8a (allergens on supplier products, the roll-up and the check) left for later** *(phase 8a shipped 27 Nov 2026, migration 316)*
- **Phase 8b, next**: a change that could alter a dish's suggestion (a preferred supplier switched, a product substituted on receiving, a recipe line or option rule changed, a product's allergens saved) doesn't reopen anything by itself: a check is worked out again only when the Allergens tab, the recipe editor or Pepper asks (and a report reads what was last worked out). 8b reopens on those changes (from the places that call `priceChanged()`, with `dishesUsing()` / `dealsHolding()` of `shared/priceCreep.js`), puts reopened checks on Today, and adds suggested calories (energy per 100 g on an item, `menu_items.calories`).
- **One check per menu dish**: the same burger on two menus is two dishes with two checks (each menu's dish is its own row); a duplicated menu's dishes start with no decisions (dismissals aren't copied with `copyRecipeLinks()`). A check is the union over every scope the dish is sold at: a menu for every venue is checked at each active venue; a revenue stream that doesn't sell the menu is still looked at through its links (stream `menu_ids` aren't read).
- **What is walked**: the dish's recipe at each scope, its prep recipes, and each offered option's rule that adds, swaps in or is a recipe; a removal, a scale or "nothing" adds no allergen. Nested options (an option's child group) are walked by their own option ids only, as costing does. Meal deals, goods and services aren't checked (a deal's allergens are its dishes'). A dish's own one-off options are matched by label, like costing.
- **What counts as unknown**: a product with no allergens saved, an item with no supplier price, a recipe with no lines, a draft Pepper read in, an option with no rule that adds something, a venue with no recipe. While anything is unknown, declared-but-in-nothing and contains-but-only-may-contain aren't said. A removable allergen ("can be removed") counts as declared; accepting never changes one to removable or back (stronger is not said for a removable one).
- **The product per venue** is `chooseQuote()`'s: a venue's choice, the group's star, else the cheapest; another supplier's product of the same item (a substitute on a delivery) isn't looked at until 8b. Products switched off, and suppliers switched off, are left out.
- **Accept and dismiss** need Recipes manage and Menus manage. Accept writes the draft through the Allergen matrix's route (one dish, every tag), never publishes; the tab says "draft not live yet" until the menu's allergens are published. A standard allergen the group has no tag for is refused (add the 14 on Menus first). There is no "accept for one venue": the declaration is the dish's. Pepper reads checks but can't accept or dismiss (a person's step on the page).
- **The spec sheet reader** reads photos and PDFs (up to 4, 20 MB together), not Word or spreadsheets; an allergen the sheet doesn't mention is "not said" and is saved as No when the reading is used (the card and the modal say so). The file is not kept. There is no live Pepper evaluation for the reader yet (the stand-in Claude tests the shape).
- **Reports** read the checks as last worked out (open the tab, or ask Pepper, to work them out again). The Allergen checks subject has no venue (a check is a dish's, across its venues). Items with unknown allergens lists products of items in any recipe, also recipes no dish uses.
