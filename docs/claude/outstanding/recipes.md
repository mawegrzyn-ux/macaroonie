# Outstanding: recipes

What is not built, known limits and follow-ups in this area (`docs/claude/areas/recipes.md`). A new item takes the next number after the highest in any file of this folder. Check the live code before calling anything here pending. The phases still to build are in `docs/INVENTORY.md` section 11.

**69. Recipes: what phase 1 left for later** *(phase 1 shipped 27 Nov 2026, migrations 298 to 301)*
- Built in phase 2 (27 Nov 2026): the links from dishes, options, meal deals and goods to recipes with the variation matrix, `margin()` in use on Menu margins, and the cost targets. Stock is still not taken off by sales (phase 5).
- Pepper can read recipes (`find_recipes`, `recipe`) but not change them: change tools (with Activity words in `shared/pepperActions.js` and a place in the registry) and the recipe reader come with phase 3.
- A unit a line uses is free text: a line can name a unit nobody has defined (it shows as a gap), and the editor's unit list offers only the units the item knows. There is no bulk "give these items a size" screen.
- The inventory import and price update don't read yield or units, and the item import doesn't set them; they are typed in the item's drawer.
- Last paid and average paid read received purchase order lines only; they don't follow stock moves, transfers or credit notes (phase 4 onwards), and the average is over a fixed 90 days.
- The Recipe costs report subject costs at standard prices only, today's; there is no history of what a recipe cost on a past day (D9: open days are recomputed, closed periods never change, comes with periods in phase 6) Periods came with phase 6a (27 Nov 2026): a closed period freezes its variance figures and its days' usage; recipe costs themselves still have no history.
- Recipe photos come from the media library through `PicturePick`, which needs the person to be able to upload to Media: a cook with Recipes manage but no media access sees the upload refused.
- No allergens, calories or labels from recipes (phase 8), and `shelf_life_hours` is kept but nothing prints a label with it.
- Recipes are per group: nothing passes recipes from a franchisor to its franchisees (phase 10).
- Pepper's link to a recipe searches the Recipes page by its name (`?q=`) instead of opening the recipe: two recipes whose names contain the same words both show. Opening one straight from a name needs a link the page resolves by name.
- After an item's base unit changes, deliveries booked in under the old unit are left out of Last paid and Average paid; the gap then says nothing was booked in. It doesn't say the old deliveries were in another unit.
- This phase made the pepper area's `api/src/ai/tools/util.js` public in `areas.json` (every area's Pepper tool file uses it; four baseline crossings went with it). It is another area's public surface: name it in the pull request's title.

**70. Recipes: what phase 2 left for later** *(phase 2 shipped 27 Nov 2026, migrations 302 and 303)*
- A meal deal's cost is its picks' sum on a sale, but on Menu margins and in reports it is a range (least and most); the typical cost from what guests actually pick waits for usage from sales (phase 5: `dishCost()` returns `typical: null` as the hook). Built 27 Nov 2026: `dishCost().typical` is the cost at `typicalPicks()` (the most picked per step from sales, else the first); Menu margins doesn't show it yet. Deals are banded at their highest cost %, each end of the range over what its own picks sell for (`dealRange()`, review fix 27 Nov 2026); a picked dish's own options that cost extra in the deal are in neither the cost nor the price of the range.
- Costs in the matrix, on Menu margins and in the Dish margins report are at standard prices only (no Last paid or Average paid switch there).
- An option rule adds one line: an option that adds two things (a "loaded" topping) needs a prep recipe holding both. Nested options (an option's child group) are costed by their own option ids only. A dish's one-off option is keyed by its label: renaming it loses its rule (the option then shows Needs a rule again).
- Rules for a library option that is deleted stay in `recipe_option_rules` unused (the option id is plain); the nightly sweep cleans links and dish rules, not those. A section's own cost target for a deleted section stays in `costing_settings.sections` until the targets are saved again.
- A goods dish's recipe is one each of its stock item: an item kept in grams with no size for each shows as a gap until it has one.
- Menu margins leave out order type charges (packaging, service), discounts and promotions; the margin is the menu price net of VAT against the dish cost. Targets are per group and per menu section, not per dish.
- The Dish margins report has a row for every live dish at each venue and active order type (no revenue stream): fine at today's sizes, heavier for many venues and big menus (one cost context per venue).
- Pepper can read links and margins (`dish_cost`, `menu_margins`) but not change them: `link_recipes` comes with phase 3 (built 27 Nov 2026). The recipe editor's Link a dish links for every venue only; other cells are set from the dish's matrix.
- No "Make a recipe for this dish" from the menu editor, no Today task or dashboard widget for dishes over target, no rule trigger on cost % yet (phase 7, `docs/INVENTORY.md` 8.2 and 10.3).
- The matrix lists active venues, streams and order types (and inactive ones a link names); a dish on a menu for one venue still shows every venue's row, marked "not on its menus".
- Dishes are costed from whichever recipe their link names, draft or live: nothing marks a dish costed from a draft (`dishCost()` returns `recipe.status`, the pages don't show it). `docs/INVENTORY.md` 5.6 means the drafts phase 3's reader makes; whether a draft link is a gap is for that phase. (Decided in phase 3: a draft Pepper wrote, `recipes.source` set, carries the gap "not checked yet"; a person's draft doesn't. See item 71.)
- `dishCost()` takes a meal deal's picks as its own list, a till line's combo parts (`modifiers`) or a basket's selection (`choices`). A stored online order's parts carry option labels, not ids (`priceLine()`): costing past orders (phase 5) needs the option ids kept on the order, or a lookup by label.

**71. Recipes: what phase 3 left for later** *(phase 3 shipped 27 Nov 2026, migration 304)*
- Coverage judges a dish's options by what it offers (`optionGaps()`), not what was picked with each sale: a burger with an unruled extra nobody orders counts as not costed. Reading the picks needs usage from sales (phase 5: till options are only in `pos_checks.state`, online options are labels, outstanding item 70). Usage from sales (27 Nov 2026) reads each sale's picks (`dish_cost_days`, the Sold tab); coverage itself still judges by what is offered.
- Coverage leaves out GloriaFood orders (by name only, no dish ids) until they go through the item mapping (phase 10), test till sales (decision D10), so a test tenant shows "no sales", and our own till's open items and online lines with no dish (no recipe can be given to them; review fix 27 Nov 2026). A group that rings much up as open items shows a high share with little behind it: the left-out value is in Pepper's answer and the report, not on the card. Another till's mapped line counts its whole value as its main dish (the extra dishes the mapping put in it aren't judged on their own). Imported sales have no order type: they are judged at the venue's own recipe.
- Coverage is worked out on each read of the Recipes page (every menu, every sold dish and scope for the window): fine at today's sizes; for ten venues and a year in the report subject it would want the sales summed by day in SQL (it is) and the costing ctx kept per run (it is), but no cache.
- The reader sends up to 800 inventory items and 300 prep recipes as refs; lines for the ones beyond them are matched in code by code or name only (never by Pepper's ref). A spreadsheet is read as rows of its first sheet only, up to 3,000 rows. Word files and HEIC photos aren't read (save as PDF or JPEG).
- A suggested match is never saved without a tap, and Pepper's `read_recipes` card can't accept one: suggested and unmatched lines go into the draft's notes, to be added on the recipe's page. There is no "add these lines from the notes" button.
- The review's Add to the inventory makes an item with a name and what it is counted in only: no category, supplier price or yield (the line then shows a gap: no price).
- A prep recipe read in the same file as a dish that uses it is matched by the model's `n` ref only, never by name in code.
- Pepper's typical yield is a suggestion from the item's name; nothing records that a yield came from Pepper once saved.
- Pepper's `make_recipe` matches ingredients by an exact name or a sure match (`AUTO_MATCH`); anything else goes into the notes (it can't add inventory items itself: `add_inventory_item` can, as its own card).
- The plain form New recipe makes a recipe's details only (its lines are added on its page or by `make_recipe`).

**78. Recipes: what phase 7a (menu engineering, scenarios, Push prices) left for later** *(phase 7a shipped 27 Nov 2026, migration 314)*
- **Phase 7b, next**: price creep (dishes whose cost % crosses a threshold when a price changes, on the page and as a Today item, on the `cost_target_states` crossing rule of 6b), cost alerts and Pepper's `why_cost_changed` (`docs/INVENTORY.md` 8.2 and 10.4).
- **Engineering's sections are the dishes that sold**: a dish on the menu that sold nothing in the period isn't one of its section's dishes (it doesn't lower the popularity share and isn't listed as a dog); look at it on the Margins tab. A row of `dish_cost_days` with any unit not costed is left out of GP whole (a day with one unruled option on one sale leaves that day's other sales of the dish out of GP too), so a dish with a few gaps reads on fewer units than it sold; the table says how many. Engineering is by the section a dish sat in when its day was worked out, with the draft's section names now; a section split or merged since reads as two. Meal deals are judged in their own section; their picks don't count in the picked dishes' sections.
- **The report subject Menu engineering has no venue**: a report works it out over every venue together (and over the first range only when compared with another period); per venue is on the page and through Pepper. The starter has no venues choice.
- **Scenarios work on the menu's draft at today's standard prices**: no last paid or average paid basis, no option or extra price changes of their own (a price change is the dish's own price and its sizes), no change to what a meal deal's picks add, and order type charges, discounts and promotions are left out (as on the Margins tab). A scenario at a venue whose menus don't include the menu has no units. The mix is units from `dish_cost_days` (usage from sales), so the last day is yesterday and a day not yet worked out has none; late sales of a closed day aren't in it (as on the Sold tab).
- **Push prices** changes one menu's draft; the same dish on another menu (each menu's dish is its own row) needs a scenario of its own. It writes over what is in the draft at the moment it saves (the preview's stamp checks the prices it changes, not the rest), like Pepper's menu tools; it never schedules a publish. There is no Undo: push the opposite change, or put the prices back on the Menus page.
- **Pepper** saves scenarios but can't push their prices or delete them (the page does); there is no evaluation task for menu engineering yet.
