# Outstanding: stock

What is not built, known limits and follow-ups in this area (`docs/claude/areas/stock.md`). A new item takes the next number after the highest in any file of this folder. Check the live code before calling anything here pending. The phases still to build are in `docs/INVENTORY.md` section 11.

**72. Stock: what phase 4a left for later** *(phase 4a shipped 27 Nov 2026, migrations 305 to 307)*
- ~~**Phase 4b, next**: waste and transfers between venues.~~ Built 27 Nov 2026 (migrations 308 and 309): item 73 lists what it left.
- **Sales aren't taken off** until usage from sales (phase 5): on hand rises with deliveries and falls only at a count, so between counts it reads high for anything used in dishes, and a count's difference includes what was sold. Every place that shows on hand says so (`NO_SALES_NOTE`).
- **A count's moment** is when it was started (or the moment given, up to 14 days back), not when it was submitted: a delivery booked in while the count is being taken counts after it, so goods counted on the shelf and booked in afterwards are counted twice until the next count. Counting before booking in, or setting the count's time, avoids it.
- **Costs at the time** are the delivery's own price, else the average paid at the venue over the 90 days before (else the last price paid): no weighted average carried through the ledger (the plan's appendix formula), and moves before the first delivery have no value.
- **A key-item list is the group's**, not a venue's, and a count follows its list as it is while counting (an item added to the list mid-count joins the sheet). There is no key list per venue, no count rota beyond one weekday per venue, and Today's count due doesn't escalate or carry to the next day.
- **No undo of an approval**: an approved count is replaced by counting again. A submitted count goes back to counting (its confirmed zeros asked again) or is cancelled.
- **The count sheet** fetches the whole count every 8 seconds while counting (fine at a few hundred items); a long count on a slow phone may want lines only. The phone page counts and submits; a submitted count's review there has Approve for whoever may (Today's cards open it there since the review fixes).
- **Areas per venue only**: copying a venue (`createStore()`, 10.5) doesn't copy its areas yet; no area template across venues.
- **Reports**: Stock adjustments have no value column (the value needs the cost at the time, worked out in code); Stock moves runs every move up to the period's end to get the balance (fine now; a levels table only if a venue's history makes it slow, as the plan says). No dashboard widget or Overview tile for stock yet (10.3).
- **Pepper** can't approve, adjust or set up areas (a person does that on the page); `count_stock` takes the person's numbers as the card shows them, with no check against the words they said. No evaluation file for stock yet (10.4).
- **Blind counts hide the count's expected figure only** (review fixes, 27 Nov 2026): the count sheet and Pepper's `count_stock` card leave it out, but on hand itself stays readable with Stock View while a blind draft is open (the On hand tab, an item's history, `stock_on_hand`, `stock_moves`, the reports), so a counter can still look the figure up. Holding those back from Use and View while the venue has a blind draft would need every reader of `loadStockOnHand()` to know about open counts; decided against for now, said in Help.
- **Who did it is kept by email** (`stockPerson()` in `routes/stock.js`: the PIN person's `users.email` on a shared device, else the account's email, else its sub): there are no `*_by_sub` columns, so a person whose sign-in email changes after counting is a different person to the second person rule for the counts they already did. A till PIN person can't count (stock routes aren't `CHECK`): counting on a shared device needs an office PIN.
- **A count dated before a later approved count** is allowed (up to 14 days back) and only warned about (`later_count` on the sheet and the review): what the later count counted keeps its figures. Nothing refuses it, since a key count of other items dated earlier still changes those items.

**73. Stock: what phase 4b left for later** *(phase 4b shipped 27 Nov 2026, migrations 308 and 309)*
- **A dish or batch is broken into items when it is logged**, by the recipe then: a recipe changed later leaves past waste as it was (on purpose: the waste happened with that recipe). A till void marked wasted is kept the first time it is read, or by the sweep within 10 minutes (`stock_void_waste`, migration 310; review fixes), and broken in again only while its till day is open (D9) or when its own record changes; its cost is the average at the void's moment, kept with it. Neither carries the "not checked yet" gap away when the draft is checked: a logged entry keeps its words, and so does a void once its day is closed.
- **Kept void breakdowns** (review fixes): a void whose dish left every menu before anything was kept for it (only within the sweep's 10 minutes, or a void older than the sweep's 14 days that nobody read) has no items until its dish is on a menu again, and isn't kept in the meantime. A void with no till day (`void_day_id` null) is final at its first read. A report run (repeatable read) breaks unkept voids in without keeping them; reads and the sweep keep them. Voids before migration 310 are kept on their first read by the recipe then (pre-production: none matter).
- **A dish with no recipe takes nothing off stock** but is still logged (and counted in the report as an entry with no item); a meal deal logged by hand has no picks, so it is a gap ("log the dishes that were in it"), while a till void of a deal is broken in by its picks. Options can't be given on the office or phone form (the till's voids carry theirs; Pepper's `log_waste` takes none either).
- **Undo and reversal**: undo is on the venue's trading day (the till's day start, else 04:00), not within minutes of logging; Pepper's card offers Undo for 10 minutes (`UNDO_MINUTES`) and calls the same route, so it fails after the day too. A reversed entry's photo stays on the original. A reversing entry is Manage only, even for the person who logged it.
- **Till voids**: matched to a reason by name only (any other void reason counts as preparation); a void typed in on the till with no dish has no items; a void of a check's whole order (Cancel order) counts when marked wasted, as the till marks it. `loadStockMoves()` reads every wasted void at the venue and, when there are any, the whole costing ctx (every menu and recipe) on each call: fine for now, a levels table or a per-request memo if a venue's history makes it slow.
- **Transfers**: one line per item; no partial receiving in two goes (received is once, for every line); no edit of a sent transfer (cancel and send again); the receiver's average paid doesn't include what arrived by transfer (`costAt()` reads deliveries only), so later moves there are valued at its own deliveries; more arriving than was sent is the receiver's gain, not a negative loss at the sender. A cancelled transfer's stock comes back at the moment it is cancelled (it was physically away): a transfer entered by mistake and cancelled after a count at the sender adds it on top of what that count found.
- **Venues per person**: the app has no list of venues per person; module levels apply at every venue of the group. The one limit is a shared device's venue (`venueProblem()` in `routes/stock.js`), which holds sending, receiving and cancelling, and (review fixes) logging, undoing, reversing and photographing waste. A per-person venue list (and with it per-venue stock levels) is a core change, not built.
- **Today** has the transfer to receive at the receiving venue only; nothing tells the sender when it is received or lost, and nothing escalates a transfer left in transit. On the phone a transfer task opens the office's Stock page (there is no phone page for transfers).
- **Pepper**: `log_waste` picks the reason by the model's choice from the group's list, and maps a dish or an item by name only (a dish on two menus with the same name is asked about); `receive_transfer` takes the person's figures with no check against their words; no rule trigger for waste over a value yet (10.4); no evaluation file for stock yet.
- **Reports**: Waste is a row per item of each entry, so "entries" is a distinct count and a value per entry needs summing its items; Stock transfers has a row at each end, so a figure over both directions counts a line twice (the measures say which direction they read). No dashboard widget for waste this week by reason yet (10.3).
