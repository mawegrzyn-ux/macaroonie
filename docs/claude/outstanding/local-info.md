**43. Local info flags and learning — follow-ups** *(shipped with migration 248)*
- Flags don't hide or filter anything: a finding flagged Not relevant still shows (hide it as before) and the next capture may find it again; flags only shape what the search is asked for next time.
- What Pepper learned is a few phrases added to the search's ask, not a filter: the search can still bring back what it was told to leave out, and nothing checks the findings against the phrases.
- Learning reads the 60 most recently flagged findings of the capture's own kind (older flags stop counting); every person's flag counts the same, whoever they are.
- Learning is per capture: the same kind of capture at several venues learns separately at each, and nothing is shared between venues or groups.
- Weather flags are shown and counted, never learned from; typed-in events take no flags.
- Hourly and recorded weather (22 Nov 2026, no migration): "recorded" is Open-Meteo's figures for the past at the venue's spot (weather models run on observations), not a weather station's readings; a day is recorded only when its capture runs after the day is over, within seven days (`WEATHER_PAST_DAYS`); history before a capture's first run goes back seven days only (Open-Meteo's archive API could fill months, not built). The hours are in the day's `data`; reports read them as Weather by the hour (`local.weather_hours`, 22 Nov 2026), but no report lines sales up with the weather hour by hour yet (the report builder's `also` joins by row, not by an hour bucket). A day's 25th hour when the clocks go back is folded into one `h`. Today's hours are only as fresh as the capture's last run (daily by default; every few hours keeps them current). Not tried against the live Open-Meteo: first live run, check a past day's hours and the Today strip. Open-Meteo's free API is for non-commercial use (see Docs, Local info).
- Tested only against a stand-in Claude: first real use, flag a handful of findings both ways on a real capture, press Learn now, read what Pepper learned and check the next run's findings. Tried in Chromium at 1280, 1015 and 390 px (a mouse, and a touch context at 390), not on a real tablet or phone.
- Bulk delete (14 Nov 2026) never touches entries that started before today (an event running since last week isn't in the days asked) and has no undo; deleted findings can come back on their capture's next run (hide them to keep them off, or turn the capture off first). The Captures window deletes one capture's findings; typed-in events and several captures at once go through Pepper (`delete_local_info`). Pepper's card lists at most the first 10 entries.
- Merging the same event (migration 249) is only as good as Pepper's reading of a few words per finding: a repeat worded very differently can be missed and, now and then, two different things put together (Not the same undoes that for good). It compares findings of one kind at one venue within a day of each other only; typed-in events and the weather are never merged, and there is no way to put two entries together by hand. Not the same is for one report and lasts for ever (it isn't per pair). Delete future entries leaves the capture's own reports that sit inside another capture's entry. A busy venue may need a few runs before everything is compared (80 findings a call, 3 calls a pass). Tested only against a stand-in Claude: first live use, run two captures of one kind at a venue and look at what was put together.
- Flag notes (migration 252): one note per person per flag, 200 characters of plain text, with no history of earlier notes; a note goes when its flag turns or comes off; at most the 5 newest notes each way per finding reach Pepper's learning, and they only shape the search's ask (nothing is filtered by them); reports don't read notes; the page shows 5 other people's notes per item (2 on the widget) and counts the rest. Tried in Chromium at 1280 px and a touch context at 390 px, not on a real tablet or phone, and never against the live model.
