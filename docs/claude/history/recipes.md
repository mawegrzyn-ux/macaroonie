# Recipes: what shipped

Completed work in this area, oldest first, as written at the time. Reference only: for how things work now read `docs/claude/areas/recipes.md` and the code. A later entry can supersede an earlier one.

New entries: a few lines each (what shipped, the migration number, the date, where the code is). No test tallies.

- ✅ **Inventory phase 1: recipes and the cost engine** *(migrations 298 to 301, 27 Nov 2026)* — the owner's ask: "inventory module - lets build it ... recipes variations, per revenue stream/order type matrix, and per location"; plan `docs/INVENTORY.md`. Items gain a yield, usual recipe and count units and their own units (`inv_items`, `inv_item_units`; the Inventory page's Units and yield card, routes in `routes/inventory.js`). Recipes and prep recipes (`recipes`, `recipe_lines`): prep nests up to five levels, a recipe inside itself is refused with its path, variations group under the recipe they vary (`variant_of`). The cost engine `shared/costing.js` (standard, last paid and average paid, yield divides, gaps with how to fix each, coverage, the explanation, `margin()`), loaded by `services/costingSvc.js`; `/api/recipes` (`routes/recipes.js`) with the editor's live preview; the Recipes page `/recipes` (`pages/Recipes.jsx`, `components/recipes/`); modules `recipes` and `costing` (group Recipes and costing); report subjects Recipes, Recipe lines and Recipe costs (a work) with two starters; Pepper's `find_recipes` and `recipe`. Not yet: links to menu dishes and the variation matrix (phase 2), see `docs/claude/outstanding/recipes.md`.
- ✅ **Inventory phase 2: dishes linked to recipes, the variation matrix, option rules and menu margins** *(migrations 302, 303, 27 Nov 2026)* — the owner's ask: "recipe variations per revenue stream / order type matrix, and per location". `recipe_links` (a dish's recipe and portions by default and per venue, revenue stream, order type or a mix; plain dish ids), `recipe_option_rules` (add, remove, swap, scale, a recipe of its own, nothing; for a library option, one dish's exception or a dish's own option by label), `costing_settings` (target and limit cost %, a section's own). Engine: `recipeFor()` (scored as `taxFor()`), `ruleFor()`, `dishCost()` (options, prefixes, meal deal picks and ranges, goods, services), `dishMargin()` / `menuMargins()`, `costBand()` in `shared/costing.js`; loaders `services/dishCostSvc.js`. Routes `/api/recipe-links`, `/api/recipe-options`, `/api/menu-margins`; the nightly `jobs/recipe-links.js`; menus' duplicate copies links (`copyRecipeLinks()`). Admin: the Recipes page's Dishes and Options tabs, the dish matrix (`components/recipes/DishMatrix.jsx`), Used on menus in the editor, the Menu margins page (`pages/MenuMargins.jsx`). Reports Recipe links and Dish margins with two starters; Pepper's `dish_cost` and `menu_margins`.
- ✅ **Inventory phase 3: recipes read in by Pepper, drafts, recipes from dishes, coverage by sales** *(migration 304, 27 Nov 2026)* — `docs/INVENTORY.md` 5.6. The recipe reader (`services/recipeReadAi.js`, `POST /api/ai/recipes/read`): photos, PDFs, Excel or CSV, pasted text; refs not ids, matched by ref, code then name, units checked, the rest listed. The Recipes page's Read recipes review (`components/recipes/RecipeReader.jsx`, lazy) makes drafts (`POST /api/recipes/drafts`); `recipes.source` and `needsCheck()`: a draft Pepper wrote is partly costed until made live. Make a recipe for this dish (`POST /api/recipe-links/dishes/:id/recipe`), a typical yield suggested in the item drawer (`POST /api/ai/recipes/yield`). Coverage by sales on the Recipes page (`salesCoverage()` in `shared/costing.js`, `services/recipeCoverageSvc.js`, `GET /api/recipes/coverage`), report subject Recipe coverage and starter Uncosted dishes by sales. Pepper's `read_recipes`, `make_recipe`, `link_recipes` (with Undo), `recipe_coverage`; the plain form New recipe; evaluation `api/eval/tasks/recipes.mjs`. Review fixes (27 Nov 2026): thousands commas in quantities (`qtyOf()`), matching past the 800 listed items, open items left out of coverage, coverage's pounds behind till reports view and its 30 days ending yesterday, one rule for offered options (`offeredOptions()`) and read lines (`readLineKept()`), whole lines in a draft's notes, the review asks before it is left. Live evaluation (`eval/run.mjs --live --only=recipes`, 27 Nov 2026): 9 of 9 tasks passed on the first trial, 21 model requests, about $0.02.
