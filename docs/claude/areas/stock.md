# Stock

Covers: stock on hand and the ledger (`shared/stock.js`, `loadStockMoves()`), storage areas, stocktakes (full and key-item counts, drafts, approval, blind counts), adjustments, waste and its reasons (with the till's voids marked wasted), transfers between venues, the Stock page and the phone's count and waste pages, the stock module, usage from sales (phase 5: the job, the Usage tab, order up to par), stock periods and variance (phase 6a: the Variance tab, close and reopen, late sales on closed days, explain_variance); 6b next (`docs/INVENTORY.md` phases 4 to 6).

Key files and mistakes to avoid for this area: read it before changing code here. What shipped and when is in `docs/claude/history/stock.md`; the index of all areas is in `CLAUDE.md`. The plan is `docs/INVENTORY.md` (phases 4 to 6 are this area's).

Moved here from purchasing on 27 Nov 2026 (inventory phase 4a): `api/src/routes/stock.js`, `api/src/services/stockSvc.js`, the stock on hand component (now `admin/src/components/stock/OnHand.jsx`), the test `api/test/stock-on-hand.test.mjs` (was `purchasing-stock-on-hand.test.mjs`) and Pepper's `stock_on_hand` / `count_stock` (now `api/src/ai/tools/stock.js`). The report files stay the reports area's, as recipes' do: `api/src/reports/catalogue/stock.js`, `work/stock.js`, `starters/stock.js` sit under `api/src/reports/**`, which a second area can't claim without `check-areas.mjs` calling the file claimed twice. `countToBase()` and `stockLow()` stay in `shared/procurement.js` (purchasing).

## Mistakes to avoid

> Append-only: never delete an entry, only correct one that turns out to be wrong. New entries go at the end.

- **Stock on hand has one rule, `stockOnHand()` in `shared/stock.js`, over the moves `loadStockMoves()` reads** (27 Nov 2026, phase 4a) — the Stock page, the item history, the count review's expected figure, the report works, Today and Pepper all go through it. Nothing stores a level, and a new way stock moves (waste, transfers in 4b, usage in phase 5) is a new entry in `MOVE_SOURCES` and, if it sets rather than adds, in `MOVE_KINDS`; never a figure worked out beside the ledger. A move at the very moment of a count is inside the count (counts sort last at a tie).
- **Only approved counts move stock** (phase 4a) — `countMoves()` reads `status = 'approved'`; a draft or a submitted count changes nothing, and an approved count is never edited or cancelled: a newer count replaces it. A full count holds a line for every item it covers (the zeros confirmed on submit are lines with `confirmed_zero`), so on hand reads lines alone, the same for full and key counts and for the counts migrated from before.
- **A count's lines are written one at a time, never as a whole count** (phase 4a; the one place Standard Design Rule 5 gives way) — `PUT /counts/:id/lines` writes one item in one area under `FOR SHARE` on the count, so two people counting two areas both land; a submit, an approval, an area done and a change of details take `FOR UPDATE`. A route that writes the count's own row under `FOR SHARE` deadlocks with another doing the same (the first version of areas done lost one of two areas); take `FOR UPDATE` for those.
- **A unit's size comes from the item's own choices, never from the browser** (phase 4a) — `entriesToBase(entries, choices)` looks each typed unit up in `countUnits()` (the standard ones, `inv_item_units`, the supplier packs) and ignores any `base_qty` sent; the entries are kept as typed with the size used. A new place that takes counted quantities (waste, transfers, Pepper) calls the same two functions, so a tray means 2.5 kg everywhere.
- **Values need Costing view, quantities don't** (phase 4a) — `stockAccess()` gives `sees_values` from `costing`; the routes leave `value`, `cost_per_base` and the review's totals out without it, and the report fields carry `needs: 'costing'`. A value is what was paid (the delivery's price, else `costAt()`'s average or last paid), so it tells supplier prices; never show it on Stock view alone.
- **Blind counts hide expected from everyone while the count is a draft** (phase 4a; corrected in the review fixes) — `countView()` sends neither `review` nor `expected` then, also to managers; the counting sheet reads each line's expected figure from `c.expected` (and checks `c.blind`), the review from `review`. A new place that shows a draft count's expected figure (Pepper, a widget) checks `blind_count` the same way: Pepper's `count_stock` card leaves its "(was ...)" out when `/meta`'s `settings.blind_count` is on.
- **Areas order the sheet, they hold no stock (D4)** (phase 4a) — an item in two areas has a line in each and the count adds them (`countReview()`, the works); deleting an area folds the lines of finished counts into the line outside an area and refuses (409 `area_in_count`) while a count being taken has lines in it. The unique key is `(count_id, item_id, area_id)` NULLS NOT DISTINCT, so moving lines to "no area" must merge, never just set `area_id` null.
- **The stock report files live under `api/src/reports/**`, which is the reports area's in `areas.json`** (phase 4a) — `check-areas.mjs` fails on a file two areas claim, so the catalogue, work and starter files stay there (as recipes' do) and the stock area's notes list them. Run `reports-catalogue`, `reports-group`, `reports-smart` and `pepper-registry` after changing them.
- **A reversal is dated at the adjustment it reverses, never now** (27 Nov 2026, review fixes) — on hand is the last approved count plus the moves after it, so a reversal dated after a count is added on top of what that count found (which already held the mistake): -2.5 kg by mistake, a count finding the real 17.5 kg, then a reversal now read 20 kg. `POST /adjustments/:id/reverse` writes the original's `adjusted_at` (when it was done is `created_at`), so the two cancel out wherever a count falls. A later way of undoing a move (waste, transfers in 4b) is dated the same way.
- **Who did it is the person, by `stockPerson(req)`, never `req.user.email`** (review fixes) — on a shared device the office PIN person switched in (`req.acting`, kept by their own `users.email` so it matches what they write from their own login), else the account. Every `_by` column takes `by`; the second person rule takes `ids` (email and sub) through `approvalProblem(count, lines, ids, settings)`, which accepts a name or a list. `whoDid()` (a display name) would make the rule compare a name with an email. Test: stock-counts' shared tablet test.
- **A delivery's value needs purchasing prices as well as Costing view** (review fixes) — a delivery's `cost_per_base` is its supplier's price and its value the line total, which purchasing and recipes' explanation hide without `seesPurchasingPrices()`. `stockAccess()` gives `sees_prices`; the moves route leaves a delivery's cost and value null without it; the report subject `stock.moves` keeps a delivery's money in its own field `paid` (measure Value received) with `needs: 'purchasing_setup'`, and `value` is empty for deliveries. Averages (on hand's value, a count difference, an adjustment) stay Costing view alone, as recipe costs do.
- **Today's count due clears only with the venue's own kind of count** (review fixes) — `GET /venues/:venueId/today`'s `done` is a submitted or approved count dated that day that is full, or of the venue's `key_list_id` on a key day; a quick count (a key count with no list: Pepper's `count_stock`, a few items) is never the day's count.
- **Submitting a count waits for the line saves** (review fixes) — `useLineSaver().flush()` returns a promise of every line sent or still saving and whether all saved; `startSubmit` and Confirm all as zero await it and stop on a failure. A fixed delay let a submit reach the server first: the late PUT then failed `mustBe(c, 'draft')` (409) and the line was dropped, or on a full count confirmed as zero. Anything that sends a count's state after lines are typed (a future Done-all, Pepper on the sheet) waits the same way.
- **An item stock has recorded is switched off, never deleted** (review fixes) — `stock_count_lines` and `stock_adjustments` cascade with `inv_items`, so `DELETE /api/inventory/items/:id` (purchasing's file) counts them as uses, as it does order and recipe lines. A new stock table keyed on the item (waste, transfers) joins that check.
- **A wasted dish or batch is broken into items by `itemsOf()` (`shared/stockItems.js`), over `dishCost()`'s lines, never by a walk of its own** (27 Nov 2026, phase 4b) — the recipe the matrix picks (`recipeFor()` with the venue, stream and order type), the options' rules, a deal's picks and each line's yield all come from the cost engine's one walk (`dishCost()`'s lines carry `yield_pct` since 4b, `flatPicks()` is exported for it). Phase 5's usage from sales takes the same function. Quantities are as bought (a line's usable quantity / yield), what stock holds; a gap is words on the entry, never a refusal: a dish with no recipe is still logged and takes nothing off.
- **`shared/stock.js` must not import `shared/costing.js`** (phase 4b) — costing imports procurement, procurement re-exports `stockOnHand()` from stock, and costing uses procurement's `UNITS` at its top level, so loading procurement first threw "Cannot access 'UNITS' before initialization". What needs the cost engine lives in `shared/stockItems.js`, which nothing in that chain imports.
- **A till void marked wasted is waste by itself, read from the till, never copied into `stock_waste`** (phase 4b, D10; corrected in the review fixes: its items are kept, see the entry below) — `loadWastedVoids()` reads `pos_check_lines` (`status = 'voided' AND void_waste`, `NOT pos_checks.is_generated`) with the line's own record from the check's state (modifiers, combo picks). It is listed read only with no undo, in the ledger as a `till_void` source, and in the Waste report. Never offer to log it again, and never let a route write a waste row for it.
- **An undo is on the venue's trading day, a reversal after it, dated with what it reverses** (phase 4b) — `wasteUndo()` is the one rule (the list's `can_undo` / `can_reverse`, the routes, Pepper's inverse): undone entries keep their row (`undone_at`) and move nothing; a reversing entry copies the items below zero at the original's `wasted_at` and `day`, as an adjustment's reversal is dated, so a count between keeps its figure. The trading day is `venueDay()` (the till's `day_starts_at`, else 04:00), so a test that logs "today" asks the API for its `today` rather than the database's date.
- **A transfer line's moves are `transferLineMoves()` only, and what didn't arrive is lost at the sender when it was sent** (phase 4b) — once received, the sender's `transfer_out` is what arrived and `lost_in_transit` the rest, both at `sent_at`, so the sender's balance is the same before and after receiving and a count there in between keeps its figure; the receiver's `transfer_in` is at `received_at`. Cancelling adds it back at `cancelled_at` (it was physically away). Never move the loss to the receiver or date it at the receipt.
- **The app has no list of venues per person: "allowed at a venue" is the group's venue and, on a shared device, the device's venue** (phase 4b) — `requireOwnVenue()` and `refuseOtherTenantsVenues` keep other groups' venues out (the body's `to_venue_id` is checked by the route too); `venueProblem(req, venueId)` refuses sending, receiving or cancelling at another venue on a shared device. If per-person venues are ever added (core), the transfer routes call `venueProblem()` already: add the check there.
- **A Pepper result's `page` carries no venue id** (phase 4b) — `log_waste` first returned `page: '/stock?tab=waste&venue=<uuid>'`, which the "names, never ids" test caught (rule 19). Link to the tab only; `open_page` takes a venue by name when one is needed.
- **The receiving and cancelling buttons follow the page's venue, not only the person's rights** (phase 4b, browser check) — with no per-person venues an owner may receive at any venue, so the sender's Transfers tab showed Receive on its own outgoing transfer. `TransferCard` shows Receive only when the transfer goes to the page's venue and Cancel only when it comes from it; the API's `can_receive` / `can_cancel` stay the rights.
- **What a till void took off is kept, never broken in afresh from today's menus once its day is closed** (27 Nov 2026, review fixes, migration 310, D9) — breaking a void in on every read used today's menus and recipes, so a seasonal dish taken off every menu (or a link changed) dropped its voids' waste out of on hand, the ledger, count reviews and the Waste report back in time. `loadWastedVoids()` keeps each void's items with their cost at the void's moment in `stock_void_waste` / `stock_void_waste_items` the first time it reads it (the sweep `jobs/stock-voids.js` within 10 minutes when nobody reads), and breaks it in again only while its till day is open, or when its own record (moment, quantity) changed; a dish on no menu now never replaces what was kept. Phase 5's usage from sales keeps its figures the same way (`stock_usage_days`).
- **Never point a foreign key at `pos_check_lines`** (review fixes) — `saveCheck()` (till's `posSvc.js`) deletes and re-inserts a check's lines on every save, so `ON DELETE CASCADE` from them wipes what hangs off a line each time the check changes. Key on the line's id with `tenant_id`, and hang the cascade on `pos_checks(id)` (only ever updated).
- **Writing while reading only in a read committed transaction that can write, under a lock, in a savepoint** (review fixes) — `keepVoidWaste()` checks `transaction_isolation` and `transaction_read_only` first: a report run's works fill in the report pool's repeatable read, where an insert racing another request's fails with a serialization error (and the run is read only after its `before`). It takes the group's advisory lock, deletes then inserts, and swallows a failure in its savepoint: a read never fails for want of keeping.
- **An upload's file extension comes from its checked type, never from the filename the client sends** (review fixes) — `/uploads/` is served on every host by `@fastify/static`, which picks the content type from the extension, and the CSP is off: a waste photo sent as `x.html` with `Content-Type: image/jpeg` was stored as an HTML page on the admin's origin, where the Auth0 tokens are in localStorage. `POST /waste/:id/photo` reads the file's first bytes (`photoType()`: JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC) and stores it under `PHOTO_EXT` of that type. Cash recon's receipt upload has the same pattern (its own area's fix).
- **A page's nav record lists every tab the page has** (review fixes) — `linkValues()` (`shared/nav/links.js`) keeps a `tab` only when the record's `tabs` has it, so Pepper's `open_page`, the registry's `openLink()` and every `pageLink()` dropped Waste and Transfers and landed on On hand. A new tab on `Stock.jsx`'s `TABS` goes into `shared/nav/stock.js`'s `tabs`, and its words into `keywords` and `description` (menu search), the same change.
- **A transfer line's cost is its own at both ends, even none** (review fixes) — `fillCosts()` fills a move with no cost from the venue's average paid; a transfer's moves carry `cost_fixed`, so a line the sender had no cost for has no value at the receiver either, never the receiver's own average.
- **What a sale takes off is `usageOf()` in `shared/costing.js`, on the cost walk's own lines, never a walk of its own** (27 Nov 2026, inventory phase 5, migration 311) — `dishCost()`'s lines carry `bought` (recipeCost()'s `bought_qty`: the item's base unit after its yield, or a prep recipe's own unit) and prep recipes inside are walked on `recipeCost()`'s lines by their yield and loss (`batchItems()`), so a dish's cost is the sum of what it takes off times each item's cost per base unit, by construction; `itemsOf()` (waste) is built on `usageOf()` / `recipeUsage()`. A change to how options, prefixes or picks act on a dish changes cost, waste and usage together: run `shared/test/stockUsage.test.mjs`, `stockWaste.test.mjs` and the costing tests together. A deal sold with no picks takes its `typicalPicks()`; waste logged by hand passes `typical: false` (a gap instead).
- **Sold lines are read once, by recipes' `loadSoldLines()` beside `loadSold()`, on the channel fragments `SOLD_FROM`** (phase 5) — what counts as sold on each channel (closed checks, lines not voided, test sales left out, the till's trading day; the counted online statuses; another till's receipts not cancelled, ignored lines out) is written in `services/recipeCoverageSvc.js` once and used by coverage, usage and the job's fingerprints. A new channel, or a change to what counts, goes there, never into a second query. Every void (marked wasted or not) is out of usage: a wasted one is waste already (`stock_void_waste`), so counting it as usage too would take it off twice.
- **A day of usage is replaced whole, in one transaction, and only an open day** (phase 5, D9) — `computeDays()` deletes and re-inserts the days' `stock_usage_days` and `dish_cost_days` and upserts their marks in the caller's transaction; the job runs each venue in its own `withSystem()` transaction under its lease, Recompute in the request's `withTenant()` after `claimVenue()`. Ask `usageHooks.isDayClosed(venueId, day)` before working a day out (phase 6 fills it); a test replaces it to check it is asked.
- **Which days the job works out again is the fingerprint, the dirty mark or force; never "every day, every run"** (phase 5) — `dayFingerprints()` per channel (lines, quantity, the last change; for other tills the parts' hash, since a re-map changes `ext_line_parts` without touching `ext_checks.updated_at`); a recipe, link, option rule or the refunds setting sets `stock_usage_venues.dirty_from` (`markUsageDirty()`), cleared by the run that claimed it only when `dirty_at` is unchanged (compared as epoch text: a JS Date drops the microseconds). A new thing that changes what a sale takes off (an item's yield, a unit) marks the days the same way.
- **The usage job claims venues, not runs: a lease per venue under `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, and a venue looked at within `AGAIN_MINUTES` isn't claimed again unless marked** (phase 5) — two API processes sweeping at once share the venues; a manual Recompute takes the same lease in its transaction (409 while the job holds it). A test that sweeps twice in a row passes `again: 0`, or the second sweep finds nothing to do.
- **On hand takes sales off at each day's last sale, up to the last run** (phase 5) — one `usage` move per item per day at `stock_usage_days.at`; a count at closing has the day inside it, one before opening has it after, one mid-trading has the whole day after it (outstanding item 75). Every answer that shows on hand carries `salesNote()` (up to the last run), never a "sales aren't taken off" note: `NO_SALES_NOTE` is gone.
- **An online order's options are words: match them within the dish's groups by `matchOptions()`, and list what doesn't match** (phase 5) — `order_items.options` and a deal part's `options` are `{ group, label }` ("2 x Cheese" is two); `usageOf()` turns them into ids and gives an `option_label` gap for one renamed since. Never cost an online option by its label alone outside that function.
- **Whole saves gained fields: `PUT /api/stock/settings` takes `refunds_return_stock`, `PUT /venues/:venueId/settings` takes `par_mode`** (phase 5, rule 13) — every caller (the Settings cards, the fixtures, the tests) sends every field; a missing one is a 400.
- **A dish taken off every menu since it sold is costed as it was last released, never dropped as 'gone'** (27 Nov 2026, inventory phase 5 review fixes) — `computeDays()` read dishes from today's live and draft menus only, so a day worked out again (any recipe, link or option rule save marks the last 14 days; a late sale; Recompute) after a seasonal dish left the menus took nothing off for it and on hand went back up for food that was used. `goneDishes()` finds a sold dish on no menu in the newest live release holding it (`menu_releases.snapshot` through `dishMap()`), else stands it in when the sale says enough (a meal deal sold with picks, a dish whose link is still kept); `left_out = 'gone'` only when no recipe is known for it. A new reader of sold dishes that works days out again later does the same, never only today's menus.
- **A dish's theoretical cost is null when none of its items had a price, never 0** (review fixes) — `dish_cost_days.cost` started at 0 and only grew for priced items, so a dish whose items all lacked a price was stored as costing £0.00 and `soldMargins()` (which counts any cost not null as costed) gave GP = net and a 0% cost on the Sold tab, the report and Pepper. `computeDays()` counts the priced items and writes null when there are none.
- **"Taken off up to the last run" is the job's last run, not the last day worked out** (review fixes) — `salesTakenOff()`'s `up_to` was the newest mark's `computed_at`, so runs that found no sales changed (nothing worked out again) left On hand and Usage showing an older time. It is the later of that and `stock_usage_venues.last_run_at` from a run without an error; a manual Recompute lets go of its lease with `releaseVenue(..., { ran: false })`, so it never moves `last_run_at` for days it didn't look at.
- **The count review's note follows what expected takes off** (review fixes) — it still said sales weren't taken off after phase 5 turned them on. A new note about expected figures says what is in them (sales by their recipes up to the last run, waste, transfers, adjustments), never what used to be missing.
- **Variance is `shared/variance.js` and only that file, over the ledger `loadStockMoves()` reads** (27 Nov 2026, inventory phase 6a, migration 312) — the Variance tab, the report works `stock_variance` / `stock_food_cost`, Today and Pepper's `variance_summary` / `explain_variance` all go through `periodFigures()` (`services/stockVarianceSvc.js`) to `itemVariance()` / `periodVariance()`. Adjustments are explained like waste (explained = waste - adjusted): with them in actual, a "found a case" adjustment showed as unexplained gain; so unexplained is the closing count against the ledger, which the property test in `shared/test/variance.test.mjs` checks.
- **A period's usage is placed as on hand places it: each trading day's move at its last sale** (phase 6a) — theoretical is the usage moves in (opening, closing], and net food sales are the `dish_cost_days` of the days whose `stock_usage_marks.last_sale_at` is in the period, so both sides of the food cost % count the same days. A count mid-trading has the whole day after it (outstanding item 75); a closed period's `days_from` / `days_to` are the days whose last sale is inside it plus the days wholly inside it (`periodDays()`).
- **A closed day is never worked out again; its late sales are kept apart and land in the next open period** (phase 6a, D9) — `runVenueUsage()` reads `closedRanges()` once and asks `usageHooks.isDayClosed(venueId, day, ranges)` per day (a test may replace it; keep it a cheap check of the ranges, never a query). A closed day whose fingerprint changed goes to `keepLateUsage()`: the day worked out now (`workDays()`, nothing written) less what was kept and what landed late before, into `stock_usage_late` with its items; the day's mark takes the new fingerprint (or the same sales would be kept again each run) and keeps its `last_sale_at`. A recipe change (dirty) never makes late sales: only sales changing does.
- **Late sales sit on the ledger at the closed day's last sale, before the period's closing count** (phase 6a) — so on hand after that count is as it was (a real late sale happened before the count, which saw the stock gone), and variance counts them in the period they landed in (`period_id`, null while it is the period running since the latest full count; `periodsForCount()` gives them to the new period when the next full count is approved), never by their moment (`late: true` keeps them out of the time-based theoretical).
- **Reopening a period works its days out again and takes its late sales home; it is refused while a later period is closed** (phase 6a) — with a later period closed, late sales that landed in it are frozen there, and working the days out again would count them twice. `POST /periods/:id/reopen` takes the usage lease (`claimVenue()`, 409 while the job holds the venue), deletes the period's days' `stock_usage_late` rows and calls `computeDays()` for them in its transaction. A note is required (`ReopenBody`).
- **Pounds need Costing view; net food sales, food cost % and an item's share of food sales need till reports view as well** (phase 6a, the Sold tab's rule) — `stockAccess().sees_sales`; the variance routes strip the money keys (`MONEY`) and `share_pct`, and give `food_cost: null`. - **Net food sales are food and drink, by the catalogue's type, never retail goods or services** (phase 6a) — `isFoodSale()` over `dish_cost_days.item_type` (the dish's `itemType()`, kept since migration 312; null for a line with no dish, counted as food).
